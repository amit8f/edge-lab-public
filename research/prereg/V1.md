# V1: known-answer tests of the data pipeline

Registered 2026-10-15, before any number below was computed on our data. Requested by Amit ("prove the
pipeline first"). V1 tests the research machinery, not a trading idea, so it does not add to the idea
count (phase 2: 13; phase 1 + phase 2: 28). Its results decide whether earlier results can be trusted.

## Question

Does our data and code reproduce numbers that someone else has already published? If it can't rebuild
the published Fama-French factors from the same months, every screen we ran (M4 to M11, P3) may be
wrong for a reason that has nothing to do with the idea it tested.

## Data

- **Our side.** Alpaca SIP daily bars (`adjustment=all` for returns, `raw` for price and market cap),
  delisted stocks kept, the same cache the screens use. Universe: every Alpaca `us_equity` asset that
  `core.instruments.classify` calls common stock on a listed exchange, mapped to a SEC CIK as in M4.
  Market cap at t: raw close x the latest cover-page shares outstanding filed on or before t,
  split-corrected (M4's method). Book equity: the latest 10-K stockholders' equity filed on or before t
  and no older than 540 days (M4's method).
- **Their side.** Kenneth French's data library: monthly Fama-French 3 factors, momentum (`Mom`) and
  short-term reversal (`ST_Rev`), downloaded at run time; each file's sha256 is saved with the results.
  French builds them from CRSP and Compustat, which we don't have. That is the point: an independent
  build from different raw sources.
- **Period.** Formation at each month-end from 2016-01-29 to 2025-08-29; returns for the following
  month (Feb 2016 to Sep 2025, 116 months). No return window reaches 2025-10-01, so the holdout stays
  untouched.

## Factor builds (fixed now; no tuning after a look at the comparison)

Monthly return = month-end adjusted close to next month-end adjusted close. A stock whose series ends
inside the month (delisting or a >15-day gap) gets its return to its last close. NYSE stocks are those
whose Alpaca `exchange` is `NYSE` (current listing; point-in-time exchange is not available, disclosed).

1. **Market.** Value-weighted (market cap at t) return of the universe minus French's `RF`.
2. **SMB and HML.** Each month-end, 2 x 3 sort: size at the NYSE median, book-to-market at the NYSE 30th
   and 70th percentiles (positive book equity only). Value-weighted portfolios. SMB = mean of the three
   small minus mean of the three big; HML = mean of the two high minus mean of the two low. French
   rebalances once a year in June with December book equity; we rebalance monthly with point-in-time
   book equity (disclosed difference).
3. **Momentum (UMD).** 2 x 3 sort on size (NYSE median) and the return from month t-11 to t-1 (skipping
   month t), NYSE 30/70 breakpoints, value-weighted; UMD = mean(small up, big up) - mean(small down,
   big down). French's `Mom` is built the same way.
4. **Short-term reversal.** As UMD but sorted on the month-t return; reversal = mean(small low, big
   low) - mean(small high, big high). French's `ST_Rev` is built the same way.
5. **MAX effect** (Bali, Cakici and Whitelaw, 2011). Deciles by the largest daily return in month t
   (NYSE breakpoints are not used, as in the paper); value-weighted next-month return of decile 10 minus
   decile 1. No French counterpart; the published answer is a negative spread (about -1% a month in
   1962-2005, smaller in later samples).

**Post-earnings drift was dropped from the list on purpose.** Later studies (Martineau, 2022) find it
has mostly disappeared since the mid-2000s in all but the smallest stocks, so a null on our data would
not tell a bug from a true answer. The French factors are sharper known answers.

## Pass criteria (all must hold)

Correlation of our monthly series with French's over the 116 months, and for the market also the mean
absolute monthly difference:

| Series | Pass if |
|---|---|
| Market minus RF | correlation >= 0.98 and mean absolute difference <= 0.50 points a month |
| SMB | correlation >= 0.85 |
| HML | correlation >= 0.70 (monthly vs annual rebalancing, and no Compustat) |
| UMD vs `Mom` | correlation >= 0.90 |
| Reversal vs `ST_Rev` | correlation >= 0.85 |
| MAX decile 10 - decile 1 | mean < 0 (sign only; 116 months can't make it significant) |

Thresholds are set from published replications on non-CRSP data, which land at 0.95-0.99 for the market
and momentum and lower for HML; ours are set a little below those because we lack CRSP's delisting
returns and Compustat's book equity.

**If any check fails, the pipeline is presumed wrong.** No further screen result is reported until the
cause is found, written up, and fixed, and every affected earlier result is rerun.

## Data reconciliation (same run)

- **Prices.** 50 (symbol, date) pairs drawn with numpy default_rng(20261015) from the universe's bars
  are compared with an independent daily price source (Stooq, or Nasdaq's historical API if Stooq is
  unreachable from the runner; the source used is recorded). Pass: at least 48 of 50 raw closes within
  1% after a documented symbol mapping. Every mismatch is listed with its cause.
- **SEC values.** 20 (CIK, concept, fiscal period) values used by the screens are drawn the same way and
  compared with SEC's Financial Statement Data Sets (`num.txt`), a separate SEC extraction from the same
  raw XBRL filings. Pass: at least 19 of 20 equal to the reported precision. Every mismatch is listed.

## Survivorship check (reported, no pass rule)

The market factor rebuilt without delisted stocks. The difference from the full build shows how much
survivorship bias the delisted names remove.
