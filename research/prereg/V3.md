# V3: can the forward test reach a verdict? (power of M8)

Registered 2026-10-15, before M8 logged its first week (the first logging day is 2026-10-16). Requested
by Amit. V3 does not add to the idea count (phase 2: 13; phase 1 + phase 2: 28). Any new forward
statistic it leads to is registered separately and counted.

## Question

How likely is M8's forward test (`research/prereg/M8.md`, amendment of 2026-10-15) to pass if the
effect M6 measured is real? And is there a statistic computable from M8's own logs with enough power to
give an answer in about two years?

## Part A: power of the test as amended (arithmetic from existing results)

Inputs: M6's per-date excess series (`research/results/M6/M6_primary_series.csv`). Steps:

1. The 6-month mean and its standard deviation, taken from non-overlapping subsamples (every 6th
   month, averaged across the 6 offsets).
2. Scale both to a 21-trading-day horizon, assuming the mean is linear in time and the variance too.
3. Compute the annualised information ratio (IR).
4. Compute the expected t-stat at 26 and 52 logged weeks, the probability of t >= 2 at each, and the
   years of forward data needed for 80% power at t >= 2.

**Disclosure:** a rough version of Part A (M6's IR about 0.4 a year, decades of data needed for 80% power)
was computed and told to Amit before this file was written. It uses only published results, so it can't
have been tuned, but it was seen.

A 1-month M6 effect has never been measured, so Part A assumes the 6-month effect scales down
linearly. Part B measures it.

## Part B: M6's ranking at 21 days, on historical weekly data

Promised by M8's amendment as post-hoc context. On the last trading day of each week from 2017-01-06 to
2025-08-29 (21-day windows ending before 2025-10-01), apply M6's exact rules (90-day Benzinga
headlines, same classifier and score, M4's universe filters). Then measure, per week:

1. **M8's statistic.** Top 20 minus the universe minus 0.2%.
2. **Rank IC.** The Spearman correlation of score with 21-day return across the whole universe.
3. **Net-direction spread.** Mean return of every stock with net score > 0 minus every stock with net
   score < 0, minus 0.2%. Stocks at zero are excluded.

For each statistic, report:

- the mean and its Newey-West t-stat (6 lags);
- the annualised IR from non-overlapping weeks (every 4th week, averaged across offsets);
- the years of forward data needed for 80% power at t >= 2.

Same-day random control for statistic 1 (seed 20261012 + date ordinal), as in M8.

## Decision rule (fixed now)

- If statistic 2 or 3 reaches 80% power within 2 years of weekly logs, it is pre-registered as a new
  counted idea. Its primary test runs on M8's logged `universe_scores` (logged from 2026-10-16 on for
  exactly this purpose) and is registered before any 21-day forward outcome exists. M8 itself is not
  changed.
- If no statistic reaches 80% power within 2 years, the report says so plainly. M8 keeps logging as a
  record, and the dashboard states the years its verdict needs.
- Either way, M8's reported numbers also gain a sequential, always-valid test (a mixture sequential
  probability ratio test with a normal mixing prior whose scale equals Part B's 21-day effect). It can
  be read every week without inflating false positives. This is context; M8's own pass rule stays as
  registered.
