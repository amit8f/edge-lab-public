## Rules (fixed now)

  1/5 of equity each), costs (0.1% a side, 3% a year borrow) and prices as K4's book C. The only difference is the

## Control and test

- **C-groq-coin**: buys or shorts the same symbols at the same moments with a coin-flip side (K4's `coin_side`).
- **Primary:** daily net return of C-groq minus C-groq-coin, Newey-West t (5 lags). Pass: mean > 0, t >= 3 and
  bootstrap 95% CI above zero after at least 120 trading days. Before then: no conclusion. 60-day look is
  exploratory only.
- **Secondary (no pass rule):** C-groq minus C, the Gemini-versus-Groq comparison Amit asked for.
