# K6: the combined design, tape plus news attention plus peers, top 100, 10 positions, $10,000 paper (main track; bot B)

Registered 2026-10-18, before any decision or return under this rule existed. Amit asked for the combined design
that I proposed (tape, news and a co-mention graph) to be built and compared with K5, and said both could run.
K6 runs as bot B beside K5 (bot A). Both are forward paper tests with their own coin-flip twins, and neither
is a finding until its own pass rule is met. Phase-2 count at registration: 29 (main 18, penny 4, news 3,
cross-track 3, crypto 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 44.

No real money. Fills are simulated in our ledger from Alpaca SIP prices, with IEX real-time prices for the current
mark (approved for bot A, 2026-10-18). Bot B has no Alpaca mirror. The mirror is display only.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

1. **Counterparty.** A stock that moves on heavy volume, is in the news, and moves with the companies it is
   named alongside is probably being pushed by information that is still spreading (the slow digestion of
   order flow, Bouchaud, Farmer & Lillo 2009). Whoever trades against that flow gives up the drift.
2. **Why a fund can't take it away.** It can, and the spread on 10 positions is the main drag.
3. **Built from:** K5's persistence of intraday moves (Gao et al. 2018), S1's relative volume, news-attention
   studies (e.g. Tetlock 2007 on media coverage, Da, Engelberg & Gao 2011 on search volume), and the co-mention
   idea of supply-chain and peer links (Cohen & Frazzini 2008 on customer links).

**Prediction written now:** B beats its coin-flip twin by less than 0.05% a day. B trades far more often than K5,
and the spread eats most of any gross edge. B's gross return is positive, its net return is not.

## Universe and data (every 10-minute run)

- The 100 most traded US stocks at $5 or more (`ai_wide.build_universe`, logged daily), with the shortable set.
- Today's 1-minute SIP bars from the open to now - 16 minutes, `adjustment=raw`. The current price is the latest
  IEX trade for each stock. A stock with no trade in the last hour keeps its last price and can't be entered.
- Alpaca news from the last 24 hours that names any of the 100 stocks. Each article's named stocks are its peers.

## Features

- `ret60`: now price / the price 60 minutes ago, from the 1-minute bars.
- `rv`: volume in the last 30 minutes / (today's volume so far / minutes so far x 30).
- `news2`: articles naming the stock in the last 2 hours. Headline wording is not read or scored.
- `peer_ret`: the mean `ret60` of the stocks named with it in the last 24 hours (needs at least one peer with a price).

## Entry, hold and exit (fixed)

- **Entry:** `rv >= 1.5`, `|ret60| >= 1%`, `news2 >= 1`, `peer_ret` has the same sign as `ret60`, and a short
  needs the stock to be shortable. Rank by `|ret60| x rv`. Take at most 2 new entries a run, with at most 5
  longs and 5 shorts open.
- **Hold:** while `rv >= 1.0` and `ret60` keeps its sign. This hysteresis cuts turnover.
- **Exit:** when it fails the hold rule, after 60 minutes, or at 15:57 (no overnight holds).
- **Size:** one tenth of equity per position. The book stops if its equity falls below 50% of its start.

## Controls and benchmarks

- **B-coin (twin):** each run, the same long and short counts as B, on random universe stocks with random sides,
  seeded by the run's key. Exits match B's.
- The comparison with K5 uses the same SPY hold book and the same costs.

## Costs

0.1% per side (`k2.FEE`), the same as K2 and K4. The round trip matches config/risk.yaml's 0.2%. Shorts pay the
same 3% a year borrow pro rata.

## Primary test (forward only)

- Daily net return of B minus B-coin, Newey-West t with 5 lags (PROTOCOL rules 11-12, `core.stats.screen_pass`).
- **Pass:** mean difference > 0, t >= 3, bootstrap 95% CI above zero, after at least 120 trading days.
  Before then, "no conclusion".
- **Comparison with K5:** reported side by side with K5's result on the same days, but each is judged on its own rule.

## Exploratory (never findings)

Turnover and cost drag; each of the three confirmations (tape, news, peers) on its own; long versus short;
hold times; the share of entries that the peer check removed.

## Holdout

Forward (paper) data only. No historical screen of this rule is run under this registration.
