# P3: short-term boom model, XGBoost on price, volume, news and analyst features (penny track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any result of this idea was computed, in the same commit as its main
track twin M10 (`research/prereg/M10.md`). Requested by Amit, who asked for short-term boom
predictions in penny and regular stocks. Phase-2 count at registration: 7 (penny 1). Phase 1 + phase 2
= 22. The penny track fits its own models on its own rows; nothing fitted on main-track data is used
here, and the reverse.

## Question

Do the 20 penny stocks an XGBoost classifier rates most likely to land in the top 2% of next-month
returns beat the equal-weight penny universe over the next 21 trading days, after penny costs?

## Universe and dates

- Universe on each signal date: raw close from $1 to under $5, median 20-day dollar volume >= $1M,
  common stocks named in SEC Form 3/4/5 filings by that date, delisted stocks kept, one row per
  company (highest dollar volume). No market-cap filter.
- Signal dates, label window, holdout guard and bar rules: as in M10 (weekly, 2017-01-06 on, next
  open to the close 21 trading days later, no window reaching 2025-10-01).

## Label, features, model

As in M10, with one change: no market-cap feature (penny market caps are often missing or stale).
Same fixed XGBoost settings, chosen before any data was seen rather than carried over from a fit; same
yearly walk-forward 2019 .. 2025.

## Primary test

Out-of-sample weekly series (2019-01-04 on): mean 21-day return of the top 20 by predicted
probability, minus the penny universe mean, minus 2.0% (penny round trip, `config/risk.yaml`). Newey-
West t with 6 lags, moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 8 weeks, 10,000 draws, seed 20261012).
**Passes** only if mean > 0, t >= 3.0 and the CI's lower bound > 0.

Penny returns have fat tails. The median weekly excess and the mean without the single best pick per
date are reported next to the primary number; a pass carried by a handful of picks is flagged for QA.

## Control

20 random penny-universe stocks per date (numpy default_rng(20261012 + date ordinal)), same costs.

## Exploratory, decided now

As M10's list (logistic, LightGBM, blend, without news/analyst, regression, top 10/50, hit rate, per
year, versus SPY, 5-day horizon, feature importance), with the short leg charged 2.0% plus 20% a year
borrow and counted only for stocks Alpaca listed as easy to borrow (today's list, so that number is
optimistic and labelled so).

## After the screen

Same as M10: the refit model's current top 20 go in the dashboard's "High risk, experimental" section
with the verdict beside them, never as Buy calls. A pass goes to its own forward pre-registration.

## Stop rule

A failed screen ends P3 as a paper-trading candidate.
