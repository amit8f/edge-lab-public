# M5: insider buying plus buybacks (main track)

Registered 2026-10-12, before any result of this idea was computed. Requested by Amit after M4 failed.
Phase-2 count at registration: 2 (main 2). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 17.

**Disclosure.** The buyback half of this score was chosen after M4's exploratory table showed net
issuance alone at +4.1% per 6 months (t 1.10) on the same 2017-2025 dates. The historical screen below
is therefore biased in M5's favour: a pass means little, a fail is informative. Only the forward test
can confirm.

## Question

Do the 20 stocks with the highest mix of recent insider open-market buying and share-count shrinkage
beat the equal-weight universe over the next 6 months, after costs?

## Universe, dates, data

Identical to M4 (`research/prereg/M4.md`): same eligibility filters, ticker -> CIK map, bars,
fundamentals, signal dates (month-ends; 2017-01-31 .. 2025-03-31 in practice, because Alpaca's SIP
history starts in 2016), entry at the next open, exit 126 trading days later, 0.2% round trip, the
2025-10-01 holdout guard. Additionally: net issuance must be defined (M4 family 5).

Insider data: SEC DERA Form 3/4/5 quarterly data sets (SUBMISSION, REPORTINGOWNER, NONDERIV_TRANS).

## Signal (no fitted parameters)

1. Insider buying at t: the dollar value (TRANS_SHARES x TRANS_PRICEPERSHARE) of non-derivative
   transactions with TRANS_CODE = "P" and TRANS_ACQUIRED_DISP_CD = "A", on original Form 4s (no
   amendments) with FILING_DATE in (t - 182 days, t], whose reporting owner is an officer or director,
   divided by M4's market cap at t. Each transaction row counts once even when a filing has several
   joint owners. Rows with a non-positive price or share count are dropped. No buys = 0.
2. Buybacks: M4's net issuance (minus 12-month log growth of split-corrected shares outstanding).

Score = mean of the two cross-sectional percentile ranks (higher insider buying and lower issuance are
better). Ties share the average rank. The 20 highest scores are the picks.

## Primary test, control, success

As M4: per date, mean 6-month return of the 20 picks minus the universe mean minus 0.2%; mean over
dates, Newey-West t with 5 lags, moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 6, 10,000 draws, seed
20261012); same-day random 20 as the control. **Screen passes** only if mean > 0, HAC t >= 2.0 and the
CI's lower bound > 0. A pass sends M5 to forward logging (confirmatory); it can only replace M4's
ranking on the dashboard after that pass.

## Exploratory, decided now

Insider buying alone; number of distinct buying insiders instead of dollar value; 12-month horizon;
top 50; excess over SPY; per year; calibration buckets for the dashboard labels.

## Stop rule

A failed screen ends M5. Its result is logged and the dashboard keeps M4's "no measured edge" label.

## Live data (only if the screen passes)

DERA data sets are published quarterly with a lag, so the nightly job would parse Form 4s from EDGAR's
daily index instead; that parser gets its own tests before it feeds the forward log.

## Data QA

Funnel counts (Form 4 purchase rows, unmapped issuers, share of the universe with any buy); the 20
largest insider-buy values hand-checked against their filings; the M4 QA list.
