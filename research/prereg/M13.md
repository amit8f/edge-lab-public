# M13: buy only above a rising 150-day average (main track)

Registered 2026-10-16, before any moving average, return or statistic of this idea was computed. It
was requested by Amit: he sent Micha.Stocks' video "6 investment methods" (2025-10-18), whose first
method ("שיטת המיכו") is to consider a stock only when it trades above its 150-day (or 200-day) moving
average and that average is flat or rising. Phase-2 count at registration: 18 (main 11). Phase 1 +
phase 2 = 33.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

1. **Counterparty.** The proposed counterparty is investors who are slow to update (anchoring,
   underreaction to news) and holders who sell winners too early and keep losers (the disposition
   effect). A stock above a rising long average has had good news that its price has only partly
   absorbed. These are the same people on the other side of price momentum.
2. **Why a $1B fund can't take it away.** It can. The rule is liquid, mechanical and decades old. This
   is the main reason to expect little. Any edge left would have to come from the rule's link to
   momentum and from crash avoidance, which big funds already trade.
3. **Published?** Yes. Brock, Lakonishok & LeBaron (1992) tested moving-average rules on the index.
   Faber (2007) tested a 10-month average for asset classes. Han, Yang & Zhou (2013) tested
   moving-average timing in volatility portfolios. Avramov, Kaplanski & Subrahmanyam (2021) found that
   the distance from moving averages predicts stock returns. Expected decay: most of the gross effect
   should be momentum, and it should be gone after the momentum factor.

**Prediction written now:** the stocks the rule allows beat the universe by about +0.0% to +0.3% a
month before costs. HAC t is under 3. Alpha on FF5 plus momentum is near 0, so the screen fails. If
the rule helps at all, it shows in the exploratory loss measures (fewer big losers), not the mean.

## Question

Each month, do the stocks the rule allows (close above its 150-day average, and that average higher
than 21 trading days earlier) beat the equal-weight universe over the next month, after costs?

## Universe, dates, data

Same panel as M3 (`research/prereg/M3.md`): M4's eligible universe on each month's last trading day,
2016-01-29 .. 2025-08-29 (116 dates). Every 1-month window ends before the 2025-10-01 holdout. The
data are Alpaca SIP daily bars. The average uses `adjustment=all` closes; universe fields use raw
prices.

## Signal (no fitted parameters)

At month-end t, for each stock with at least 171 bars in its current series:
- MA150(t) = mean of the last 150 adjusted closes up to and including t.
- **Allowed** = close(t) > MA150(t) **and** MA150(t) > MA150(21 trading days before t).

All allowed stocks are held, equal weight.

## Entry, exit, costs

Entry is at the next trading day's open. Exit is at the close of the next month's last trading day. A
series that ends early exits at its last close. Cost: 0.2% round trip (config/risk.yaml, main track)
times the month's turnover. Turnover is the share of this month's allowed stocks that were not
allowed at the previous date; the first date counts as 100%.

## Primary test

For each t: (mean return of the allowed stocks) minus (mean return of the whole eligible universe)
minus cost. The statistic is the mean over the 116 months, with a Newey-West t (3 lags) and a
moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 6 months, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261016).

**The screen passes** (PROTOCOL rules 11, 12 and 14) only if all of these hold:
- the mean is > 0;
- the HAC t is >= 3.0;
- the CI's lower bound is > 0;
- the monthly alpha on the Fama-French 5 factors plus momentum is > 0 with t >= 2.0;
- M3's pipeline replication (Ken French's momentum and market factors) passes on this panel.

It also reports the deflated Sharpe ratio, with the IDEAS.md phase 1 + phase 2 count as trials.

## Control

The control is a same-day random set of the same size as the allowed set, drawn from the same
universe (numpy default_rng(20261016 + date ordinal)), with the same turnover cost.

## Exploratory, decided now (never reported as findings)

- 200-day average instead of 150.
- "Above" without "rising".
- 20-day average (Micha's method 2).
- Allowed minus not-allowed spread.
- 3-month hold.
- Per year.
- Share of picks falling 20%+ in the month, for allowed vs universe.
- Worst month.
- Top 25 allowed stocks by distance above MA150.
- The primary rule with plain 12-1 momentum added as a control in the regression.

## Forward test (only after a screen pass)

From the first month-end after a pass, log the allowed set and its random control each month-end in
the hash-chained ledger. After 12 logged months, run the primary statistic on the forward months. No
real money.

## Stop rule

A failed screen ends M13. A pass in an exploratory variant is a new idea, not a result.
