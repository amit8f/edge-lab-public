# E1: macro-release blackout filter on existing trade lists (cross-track)

Registered 2026-10-20, before any result of this idea was computed. Requested by Amit after a TikTok post
describing an "AI agent team" whose news-and-timing agent blocks entries around CPI releases. Of the seven
agents in that post, this is the only rule we don't already have. Phase-2 count at registration: 32
(cross-track 4). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 47.

This idea uses other tracks' logged outputs (PROTOCOL: allowed only when a pre-registration says so). It
reads finished screens' trade-level files and changes none of them.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- Other side: on macro release days, prices move on the release for the whole market. A stock-specific
  entry that day carries extra market noise and wider spreads in the first minutes, so the stock-specific
  signal is a smaller part of the trade's outcome.
- Why a $1B fund can't take it: it isn't an edge to take; it's a filter that may lower cost and noise for
  small entries at the open.
- Published: macro-day effects on the market are well documented (Savor & Wilson 2013, higher market
  returns on announcement days; Lucca & Moench 2015, pre-FOMC drift). Our prediction: no reliable harm to
  stock-specific trades held for days; if anything, macro-day entries earn slightly more (Savor & Wilson).

## Question

For trades in finished historical screens, are trades entered on a major US macro release day worse after
costs than the same idea's other trades?

## Data and dates

- Trade lists: every finished screen with a trade-level file. At registration that is
  `research/results/P4/P4_trades.csv` (column `entry_day`, return `gross` less P4's registered costs).
  Trade lists from later screens are added only if they finish before E1 is run; none are added after.
- Entry dates 2016-01-01 to 2025-09-30. Holdout (label dates from 2025-10-01) untouched.
- Macro calendar: CPI release days and Employment Situation release days (BLS release archives) and FOMC
  statement days (federalreserve.gov calendars), compiled and committed as
  `research/results/E1/macro_days.csv` before any trade return is joined to it.

## Primary test

Within each idea, demean trade returns by the idea's mean. Pooled difference: mean demeaned return of
macro-day entries minus non-macro entries, with standard errors clustered by entry date. Pass (filter
helps): difference < 0 with |t| >= 3 and at least 100 macro-day trades. Per-idea results reported too.

Secondary (descriptive only): CPI, jobs and FOMC days separately; the day before each release.

## What a result does

- Pass: the filter may be added only as a new forward paper test with its own registration. Live P1/P2
  rules are not changed.
- Fail: no blackout filter. The result stays in IDEAS.md.
