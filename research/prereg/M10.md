# M10: short-term boom model, XGBoost on price, volume, news and analyst features (main track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any result of this idea was computed. Requested by Amit ("a section of
risk taking trying to predict stocks that will boom in the short term ... XGBoost ... with, without,
multiple"). Phase-2 count at registration: 6 (main 6). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 21. Its penny twin is P3,
registered in the same commit; the two share code for features but never data rows, fitted models or
results.

## Question

Do the 20 stocks an XGBoost classifier rates most likely to land in the top 2% of next-month returns
beat the equal-weight universe over the next 21 trading days, after costs, when every model is trained
only on data that was complete before it predicts?

## Universe and dates

- Universe on each signal date: M4's eligibility (`research/prereg/M4.md`): raw close >= $5, market cap
  >= $300M (SEC shares counted from their filing date), median 20-day dollar volume >= $2M, one row per
  company, common stocks named in SEC Form 3/4/5 filings by that date, delisted stocks kept.
- Signal dates: the last trading day of every week (usually Friday), 2017-01-06 onward.
- Label window: entry at the next open, exit at the close 21 trading days after the signal date (or
  the series' last close when it ends first). No label window may reach 2025-10-01 (phase-2 holdout).
- Bars: Alpaca SIP, `adjustment=all` for returns, `raw` for price and dollar volume; bars from
  2015-06-01, >15-day gaps end a series, zero-volume bars dropped.

## Label

y = 1 when the stock's 21-day return is in the top 2% of that signal date's universe, else 0.

## Features (fixed now; each is turned into a percentile rank within its signal date)

Price and volume, all from bars up to the signal date's close: returns over 1, 5, 21, 63 and 126
days; 12-month momentum skipping the last month; close / 252-day high; close / 252-day low; volatility
of daily log returns over 21 and 63 days and their ratio; the largest daily return in the last 21 days;
the largest absolute open gap in the last 5 days; share of up days in the last 21; 14-day RSI; 5-day
over 63-day average dollar volume; log median 20-day dollar volume; log raw price; log market cap.

News and analyst (Benzinga via Alpaca, single-symbol headlines only, created by the signal date's
23:59 ET): headline count in the last 7 and 30 days; M6's upgrade/raise count, downgrade/cut count and
net score over the last 90 days (M6's fixed rules). Headlines are fetched only up to 2025-09-30.

## Models (hyperparameters fixed now, no tuning)

Primary: XGBoost classifier, 400 trees, max depth 4, learning rate 0.05, subsample 0.8, column
sample 0.8, min child weight 20, `hist` tree method, seed 20261015, missing values left to the trees.

Walk-forward: one model per calendar year 2019 .. 2025, each trained on every signal date whose label
window closed before that year's first signal date, then used for that year's signal dates. So no
prediction uses a label it could not have known.

## Primary test

For each out-of-sample signal date (2019-01-04 .. the last date whose window ends before 2025-10-01):
mean 21-day return of the 20 highest-probability stocks, minus the universe mean, minus 0.2% (main
round trip, `config/risk.yaml`). Statistic over the weekly series: mean, Newey-West t with 6 lags
(windows overlap about 4 weeks), moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 8 weeks, 10,000 draws, seed
20261012). **Passes** only if mean > 0, t >= 3.0 and the CI's lower bound > 0. The bar is t >= 3, not
2, because this is the project's 21st idea and a flexible model.

## Control

Same dates, 20 stocks drawn at random from the same universe (numpy default_rng(20261012 + date
ordinal)), same costs and statistic.

## Exploratory, decided now

Logistic regression on the same ranks (missing set to 0.5, C = 1.0); LightGBM with the same tree
count, depth, rate and sampling; a blend (mean of the three models' within-date ranks); XGBoost without
the news and analyst features; XGBoost regression on the within-date return rank; top 10 and top 50;
hit rate (share of picks that land in the top 2%, against 2% by chance); per year; versus SPY; the
bottom 20 as a short leg (minus 0.2% and 3% a year borrow); 5-day horizon; feature importance (gain).
The top 20 picks of the primary model on the last screen date are listed for a hand check against raw
bars.

## After the screen

The final model is refit on every label that closed before 2025-10-01 and saved with the results.
Whatever the verdict, its current top 20 are shown on the dashboard in a section labelled "High risk,
experimental", with the screen's result next to them and never as Buy calls. A pass sends M10 to a
forward paper test with its own pre-registration; a fail is shown as a fail.

## Stop rule

A failed screen ends M10 as a candidate for paper trading. The dashboard section stays only because
Amit asked to see the model's picks, and it says the model failed.
