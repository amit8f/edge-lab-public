# K1: an AI crypto trader with $100, paper only (crypto, outside the three tracks)

Registered 2026-10-16, before the bot made any decision or any return existed. Amit asked for it after
watching @laeshaai's TikTok "I gave Claude $100 to trade" (a Claude bot on BTC/ETH/SOL with 5x leverage,
2 trades, +$15 on a paper account): "build it also in a paper trading with 100$ and see how its doing,
add stuff that you think could help, and let's test it". Crypto is not one of the three tracks in
CLAUDE.md; K1 is a separate, self-contained test at the owner's request and shares only `core/ledger.py`
and the state script. Phase-2 count at registration: 19 (main 11, penny 4, news 2, cross-track 1,
crypto 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 34.

No real money, ever. Prices are read from Kraken's public API; nothing is sent to an exchange.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

1. **Counterparty.** If a language model reading the last day of hourly candles can call direction,
   it is taking money from other short-term traders on the same public candles. Nobody obviously slow
   or forced is on the other side.
2. **Why a $1B fund can't take it away.** Nothing stops them. BTC, ETH and SOL are among the most
   watched, most algorithmically traded markets there are.
3. **Published?** Lopez-Lira & Tang (2023) found ChatGPT adds information when reading news
   headlines, not price charts. LLM trading agents that read prices (FinMem and similar) report
   backtests on periods the models may have seen in training. Hourly crypto direction is close to a
   coin flip.

**Prediction written now:** both AI books end within the middle 90% of their coin-flip twins (no
evidence of skill). Book A (5x) is more likely than not to be liquidated at least once in 180 days and
to end below $100 after fees and funding.

## Books (all start with $100 on the first run)

| Book | What it is |
|---|---|
| A | **Her setup.** Gemini 2.5 Flash (free key, temperature 0) reads the last 24 one-hour and 12 four-hour closed candles for BTC, ETH and SOL plus its open position, and answers long/short/close/hold for one symbol with one line of reasoning. One position at a time; 25% of equity as margin; 5x isolated leverage; no stop. |
| B | **Our changes.** Same model and candles, plus the last 30 daily candles, 2x leverage, a 5% price stop, and an instruction that holding cash is a good answer unless it sees a real edge. |
| A-coin, B-coin | **Coin-flip twins.** Open and close on the same symbol at the same moment as A or B, with the side chosen by a seeded random draw. They show live what luck alone looks like. |
| HOLD | Buys $100 of BTC on the first run and never trades. |
| TREND | No AI: holds BTC (1x) while BTC's daily close is above its 20-day average, otherwise cash. A dumb rule to beat. |

The model was trained before every date in the test (Gemini 2.5 Flash's cutoff is 2025-01-31), so
the CLAUDE.md rule on LLM outputs holds: all data is forward.

## Execution and costs (override `config/risk.yaml`, which has no crypto section; added there as `crypto`)

- Runs hourly (GitHub Actions, minute 7). Fills at Kraken's last trade price when the run happens.
- Fee 0.05% of notional on each side (her number; Kraken Futures taker is about the same).
- Funding: 0.01% of notional per 8 hours held, charged to longs and shorts alike (conservative).
- Isolated margin: a position is liquidated when its loss reaches its margin minus 0.5% of notional
  (maintenance), checked against every closed 1-hour candle's high and low since the last run. The
  whole margin is lost. B's 5% stop is checked the same way and fills at the stop price (no gap
  modelling beyond that; a candle that hits both stop and liquidation counts as liquidation).
- A failed or unreadable model answer is "hold" and is logged as such.
- The model's raw answer, the prompt's SHA-256, the candles it saw and every fill are logged hourly in
  the hash-chained ledger (`state-crypto` branch, `crypto/ledger.jsonl`). The book file itself is
  rewritable state; the ledger is the record.

## Primary test (on 2027-04-14, 180 days after the first run)

For each AI book (A, B): take its logged round trips (symbol, open hour, close hour) and replay them
10,000 times with each trip's side drawn at random (seed 20261016), using the logged hourly candles,
the same sizing, leverage, stop, liquidation, fees and funding. p = share of replays whose final equity
is at least the book's actual final equity. **Skill is shown** only if the book made at least 30 round
trips and p < 0.05 after a Holm correction across A and B. Fewer than 30 round trips: "no conclusion".

This tests whether the AI picks the right *direction*; it holds the AI's timing and symbol fixed. It is
the right comparison because timing without direction makes no money on average.

## Secondary and exploratory (decided now, never reported as findings)

Final equity of every book against HOLD, TREND and cash; maximum drawdown; number of liquidations;
win rate; average hold; share of hours in a position; equity at 30, 60 and 90 days; how often the two
AI books agree.

## Stop rule

If a book's equity falls below $5 it stops trading and its result is final. Otherwise the test ends at
180 days. A pass is a hypothesis for a new pre-registered forward test with different coins, not a
reason to trade real money (which needs the owner's written change to PROTOCOL.md).
