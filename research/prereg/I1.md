# I1: penny stocks catch up with their industry's big stocks (penny track)

Registered 2026-10-23, before any of this idea's data was pulled or computed. Amit's pick (top 3 of
`planning/BOOKS_REVIEW_2026-10-23.md` in the project files). Phase-2 count at registration: 41 (main 20,
penny 6, news 7, cross-track 7, crypto 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 56.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- **Counterparty:** holders of small stocks who react late to industry news because few analysts,
  funds or headlines cover their names. News about an industry shows up first in its big stocks and
  reaches the small ones over days (Lo & MacKinlay 1990, *A Non-Random Walk Down Wall Street*; Hou 2007,
  who finds the lag works within industries). The seller is whoever sells the small stock at a price
  that hasn't caught up yet.
- **Why us:** $1-5 stocks trading $500k-$5M a day can't absorb a fund's money; ours is a few hundred
  dollars per name.
- **Decay:** published 1990 and 2007, mostly in weekly returns. The 2% penny round trip is the problem:
  a weekly hold must earn over 2% more than the control. Prediction written now: about 75% this fails,
  most likely on costs.

## Universe and dates

- Signal day t = the last trading day of each week, from the first week with 60 bars (2016) to the last
  week whose exit is before 2025-10-01.
- Big stocks on t: M4's universe (`research/prereg/M4.md`). Small stocks on t: P1's penny universe
  (`research/prereg/P1.md`: common stock, raw close $1-5, median 20-day raw dollar volume >= $500k,
  >= 60 bars).
- Industry: French's 12 industries from each company's EDGAR SIC code (`core/industry.py`). The SIC is
  the current one, not point-in-time; companies that changed industry are rare and this is stated now.
  Industry 12 (Other) is left out.
- Industry move: the equal-weighted 5-day adjusted return (close t over close t-5, `adjustment=all`) of
  its big stocks, minus SPY's. An industry needs at least 10 big stocks on t.
- Signal: the industries with the top 2 moves on t, if the move is at least +2%. Picks: their small
  stocks whose own 5-day return is below the industry move (they haven't caught up), at most 20 per
  week by the largest gap.

## Trade and control

Equal weight, long only (penny shorts can't be checked for borrow on past dates). Entry at the next
trading day's open; exit at the close of the 5th trading day from entry. Net return = adjusted exit
close over adjusted entry open, minus 1, minus the 2% penny round trip (`config/risk.yaml`). A stock with
no entry bar is dropped; one that stops trading before exit is closed at its last close and kept.

Control: each week, as many small stocks as there are picks, drawn at random (numpy
default_rng(20261024 + week index)) from the whole penny universe on t, traded the same way. A week with
no signal is skipped and counted.

## Primary test

Per signal week: mean net return of the picks minus the control's. Over all signal weeks: the mean, a
Newey-West t (4 lags), a 95% block-bootstrap CI (blocks of 4 weeks, 10,000 draws, seed 20261024).
**Pass** only if the mean > 0, t >= 3.0, the CI's lower bound > 0, and FF5 + momentum alpha of the
weekly series > 0 with t >= 2.0 (daily French factors summed over each window, Newey-West). Deflated
Sharpe with 56 trials.

A failure ends I1. A pass sends I1 to a Plan v3 shadow book with its own forward rule.

## Pipeline check (PROTOCOL rule 14)

Same SIP bars and universe code as M3 and T1, so it counts only if M3's replication passes. 20 random
picks are checked by hand (industry, industry move, own return, prices) in
`research/results/I1/I1_rows_check.csv` before the result is reported.
