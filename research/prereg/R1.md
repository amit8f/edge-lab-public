# R1: buy last week's biggest losers that are still in an uptrend (main track)

Registered 2026-10-23, before any of this idea's data was pulled or computed. Amit's pick (top 3 of
`planning/BOOKS_REVIEW_2026-10-23.md` in the project files). Phase-2 count at registration: 40 (main 20,
penny 5, news 7, cross-track 7, crypto 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 55.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- **Counterparty:** traders who sell in a hurry after a sharp drop (stop-outs, margin calls, funds
  cutting losers, overreaction to a headline). Buying from them is paid like a market maker is paid
  for taking the other side (Jegadeesh 1990; Lehmann 1990; Nagel 2012 shows the return is a payment for
  liquidity and is largest when markets are stressed). The 200-day filter follows Connors & Alvarez
  (*Short Term Trading Strategies That Work*): the drop is a pullback in a stock that is otherwise rising.
- **Why us:** in $300M+ stocks, statistical-arbitrage desks already run this at scale, so the edge
  left is small. Our only advantage is size: a few thousand dollars pays no market impact.
- **Decay:** published 1990 and traded heavily since. Expect most of it gone in large stocks; a small
  remnant in the $300M-$2B names may survive costs. Prediction written now: about 70% this fails.

## Universe and dates

- Signal day t = the last trading day of each week, from the first week where the universe below has
  200 bars (late 2016) to the last week whose exit is before 2025-10-01.
- Universe on t: M4's universe (`research/prereg/M4.md`: common stock, delisted kept, CIK-mapped, raw
  close >= $5, market cap >= $300M, median 20-day raw dollar volume >= $2M, >= 253 bars in the series).
- Losers: stocks whose adjusted close (`adjustment=all`) on t is above their 200-day simple average of
  adjusted closes, and whose 5-day return (close t over close t-5) is in the bottom decile of the whole
  universe on t (ranked; ties by symbol). At most 20 per week: if more qualify, the 20 with the lowest
  5-day return.

## Trade and control

Equal weight, long only. Entry at the next trading day's open; exit at the close of the 5th trading day
counted from entry. Net return = adjusted exit close over adjusted entry open, minus 1, minus the main
round trip of 0.2% (`config/risk.yaml`). A stock with no entry bar is dropped; one that stops trading
before exit is closed at its last close and kept. No stop (a 5-day hold; the stop is left to the forward
book if R1 passes).

Control: each week, as many stocks as there are losers, drawn at random (numpy
default_rng(20261023 + week index)) from the rest of the universe on t, traded the same way. A week
with no losers is skipped and counted.

## Primary test

Per week: mean net return of the losers minus mean net return of the control. Over all weeks: the mean,
a Newey-West t-statistic (4 lags) and a 95% block-bootstrap CI (blocks of 4 weeks, 10,000 draws, seed
20261023). **Pass** (PROTOCOL rules 11-12) only if the mean > 0, t >= 3.0, the CI's lower bound > 0,
and the weekly losers-minus-control series has FF5 + momentum alpha > 0 with t >= 2.0 (daily French
factors summed over each holding window, `core/stats`, Newey-West errors). The deflated Sharpe ratio
uses 55 trials.

A failure ends R1. A pass sends R1 to a Plan v3 shadow book (PROTOCOL rule 17) with its own forward rule.

## Pipeline check (PROTOCOL rule 14)

R1 uses M3's Alpaca SIP daily bars, asset list and M4's universe code, so it counts only if M3's
replication (`research/REPLICATION.md`) passes. 20 random loser-weeks are checked by hand against an
independent bar pull (5-day return, 200-day average, entry and exit prices) in
`research/results/R1/R1_rows_check.csv` before the result is reported.

## Reported alongside (not tests)

The same numbers before costs; costs measured from SIP quotes (`core/costs.py`) on 300 random trades;
results split by market cap ($300M-$2B vs larger) and by whether the stock had an 8-K in the 5 days to
t; the same rule on P1's penny universe with the 2% penny round trip.
