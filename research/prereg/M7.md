# M7: earnings surprises from SEC filings (main track)

Registered 2026-10-12, before any result of this idea was computed. Requested by Amit ("Both, in
order": analyst changes first, then earnings surprises) as a free signal. Phase-2 count at
registration: 4 (main 4). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 19.

## Question

Do the 20 stocks with the largest recent standardized earnings surprise beat the equal-weight universe
over the next 6 months, after costs?

## Universe, dates, costs, test

Identical to M4 (`research/prereg/M4.md`): same eligibility, entry at the next open, 6-month exit, 0.2%
round trip, signal dates 2017-01-31 .. 2025-03-31, the 2025-10-01 holdout guard, the same primary
statistic (HAC t with 5 lags, moving-block bootstrap, seed 20261012), the same random control and pass
rule (mean > 0, HAC t >= 2.0, bootstrap CI lower bound > 0). Additionally, the stock must have a
defined surprise (below).

## Data

SEC companyfacts (the M4 download), net income (`NetIncomeLoss`, else `ProfitLoss`), original values
only: each fiscal quarter keeps the value from the first filing that reported it, usable from that
filing date.

- Quarter q's net income: a 10-Q or 10-K fact covering 80-100 days ending on q's end date. When a
  fourth quarter is not reported directly, it is the annual value (330-400 days) minus the nine-month
  value (260-290 days) ending on the third quarter's end, both first-filed, usable from the 10-K's
  filing date.
- Share-count scale errors are dropped as in `fundamentals.drop_scale_errors`; net income is not
  per-share, so splits do not matter.

## Signal (no fitted parameters)

For each company and quarter q: change = NI(q) - NI(q - 4), where q - 4 is the quarter ending 345-385
days earlier. SUE(q) = change(q) / standard deviation of the previous 8 such changes (at least 4
required; standard deviation must be > 0). At signal date t the stock's SUE is that of its latest
quarter whose first filing date is in (t - 120 days, t]; otherwise undefined. The 20 highest SUEs are
the picks (ties: random with the control's seed).

## Exploratory, decided now

SUE deciles (monotonicity); 12-month horizon; top 50; excess over SPY; per year; filing-window
reaction (the stock's return from the close before the filing date to 2 trading days after, minus
SPY's) as its own ranking and averaged with the SUE rank; calibration buckets.

## Stop rule

A failed screen ends M7; the result is logged and the dashboard does not change. A pass sends M7 to
forward paper logging.

## Data QA

Funnel (companies and quarters with SUE, share of universe covered); the 20 largest |SUE| values
hand-checked against the filings; a check that no SUE uses a filing dated after t; the M4 QA list.
