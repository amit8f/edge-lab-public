# N1: two LLMs label headlines; short the bad news (news track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any headline was labelled by a model and before any return of this idea
was computed. From plan v2. Phase-2 count at registration: 7 (main 6, news 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 22.

## Question

When two independent LLMs both read a company headline as materially negative, does the stock keep
falling after the next open, for 3 days, by more than costs? (Lopez-Lira & Tang found the edge of LLM
headline scores sits mostly in the short leg; the news is often priced in the gap, so entry here is at
the first open we could actually trade.)

## Models

Two free-tier models from different families: one Gemini Flash model (Google AI Studio) and one Llama
model served by Groq. Before any label is computed, the exact model IDs and each model's published
training cutoff are written to `research/results/N1/models.json` and committed. Temperature 0. The
same two models are used for the screen and the forward test; if one is retired, its replacement is a
new idea.

## Prompt (fixed)

System: "You label financial news headlines. For each numbered headline about the named company,
answer NEG if it is clearly bad news for that company's share price, POS if clearly good news, NEU
otherwise (routine, mixed, unclear, or about someone else). Answer only with lines 'number: LABEL'."
User: numbered lines "i. [SYMBOL] headline", up to 50 per request. A missing or malformed answer
counts as NEU.

## Universe, headlines

Headlines: Benzinga via Alpaca's news API that name exactly one symbol; exact repeats of the same
headline for the same symbol within 3 days count once. The stock must be in M4's main universe
(`research/prereg/M4.md`) on the trading day before the headline.

## Screen window

Headlines created from the first day of the month after the later of the two models' training
cutoffs, through 2025-09-24 (every 3-day exit ends before 2025-10-01). If that window is shorter than
3 months, the screen is skipped and N1 goes straight to the forward test.

## Signal, entry, exit, costs

A short signal is a headline both models label NEG. Entry at the first regular-session open at least
30 minutes after the headline's created_at; exit at the close of the 3rd trading day (the entry day is
day 1), `adjustment=all`. One trade per stock per entry day; a stock with both NEG and POS-by-both
headlines for the same entry day is skipped. Costs from `config/risk.yaml` (news): 0.2% round trip,
borrow 3% a year for 3 days. The screen uses the time exit only; stops apply in the forward paper book.

## Primary test

Trade return = -(stock return - SPY return over the same open-to-close window) - costs. Per entry day:
the mean over that day's trades. Statistic: the mean over entry days, Newey-West t with 2 lags, and a
moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 5 days, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261015).

**Screen passes** only if there are at least 200 trades, the mean > 0, the HAC t >= 2.0 and the CI's
lower bound > 0. The screen can only reject.

## Control

For each trade, a stock drawn at random from the same day's universe (numpy default_rng(20261015 + date
ordinal)), shorted with the same timing and costs. Expected near minus costs; a control clearly away
from that means a data bug.

## Label check

Before returns are computed: 100 random labelled headlines are printed with both labels and checked by
hand; the agreement rate between the models and with the hand labels is reported. A bug found here
(e.g. answers matched to the wrong headline) can be fixed and logged; the prompt cannot change.

## Exploratory, decided now (never reported as findings)

The long leg (both POS); each model alone; headlines outside vs inside market hours; 1-day and 5-day
holds; trades with the paper book's ATR stops; per month.

## Forward test (confirmatory)

After a screen pass, or when the screen is skipped: a job before each open labels the headlines logged
since the previous run (`tracks/news/collect.py`) and sends short signals to the paper book under the
news track's risk rules (2.5 x ATR stop capped at +15%, day-3 time exit, at most 5 positions; a short
needs the stock to be shortable and easy to borrow). When a scheduled run is skipped, entry is at the
first open after the run that saw the headline, and that delay is logged. After 100 closed trades the
primary statistic is computed on the forward trades. No real money.

## Stop rule

A failed screen ends N1. The long leg is not tested as a primary on its own unless registered as a new
idea.

## Clarifications (2026-10-15, before any label or return was computed)

1. "M4's main universe" means M4's eligibility rules as listed in `research/prereg/M1.md` (common stock,
   CIK mapped, raw close >= $5, market cap >= $300M, median 20-day dollar volume >= $2M, 253 bars in
   the current series, one line per company). M4's "4 of 6 factor families" rule is specific to M4's
   score and is not used.
2. The control's seed uses the trade's entry day: default_rng(20261015 + entry-day ordinal).
3. The models are fixed in `research/results/N1/models.json` (Gemini 2.5 Flash, cutoff January 2025;
   Llama 4 Scout on Groq, cutoff August 2024), so the screen window is 2025-02-01 .. 2025-09-24.
4. The hand check of 100 labels is done by Claude and committed as `research/results/N1/label_check.md`
   before the returns stage can run.

## Amendment (2026-10-15, before any result of this idea exists)

Amit approved the project-wide rules 10-15 in `research/PROTOCOL.md`. For this screen that means:
- The pass bar is a HAC t >= 3.0 (was 2.0), with the other conditions unchanged.
- It must also show a monthly alpha > 0 with t >= 2.0 on the Fama-French 5 factors plus momentum
  (Newey-West errors, same lags as the primary test; `core/stats.py`).
- It reports the deflated Sharpe ratio, with the phase 1 + phase 2 idea count from `research/IDEAS.md`
  as the number of trials.
The counts written above are superseded by the IDEAS.md header at result time.
- For N1 the factor regression is on the per-entry-day series; each day's factors are the daily
  Fama-French 5 + momentum returns summed over the trade's 3 days (close to close, an approximation of
  the open-to-close window).

## Amendment (2026-10-15, before any label or return was computed): dictionary baseline

Amit approved the deeper plan's rule that every LLM signal is compared with the Loughran-McDonald
dictionary. N1's result also reports, beside the primary test and without changing its decision:
- The same short trade (timing, costs, one trade per stock per entry day) on headlines the dictionary
  labels NEG: more LM negative words than positive ones, at least one (`tracks/news/lm.py`, word lists
  in `tracks/news/lm_words.json` with their source and sha256; whole words, no negation handling).
- The per-entry-day difference, LLM shorts minus dictionary shorts, on days both trade (Newey-West t,
  2 lags), and the trades only the LLMs take and only the dictionary takes.
If the LLM shorts don't beat the dictionary's, the report says the LLMs add nothing measurable here.
