# G1: linked companies, customer momentum and the per-stock link card (main track)

Registered 2026-10-17, before any of this idea's data was downloaded. Amit's request: surface what passes
between companies, and between the people tied to them, that helps read a stock's trend. Only public
records are used (SEC filings and published news); nothing private about any person is collected.
Phase-2 count at registration: 24 (main 15, penny 4, news 2, cross-track 2, crypto 1). Phase 1 + phase 2
= 39.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- **Counterparty:** investors in a supplier who don't follow its big customers. When a big customer's
  stock moves on news, the supplier's price catches up only over the next month (Cohen & Frazzini 2008,
  "Economic Links and Predictable Returns", Journal of Finance: about 1.5% a month long-short in
  1981-2004). Shared directors and news co-mentions are looser versions of the same slow spread of
  information (Ali & Hirshleifer 2020 on connected firms).
- **Why us:** the link has to be read out of 10-K text by hand or by code; most investors never do it.
- **Decay:** the paper is famous and later work finds the effect smaller after 2008. Expect a fraction
  of the old number; a fail is the likely outcome and is still worth knowing.

## Links (all point-in-time, each counted from its filing or publication date)

1. **Customer links (primary).** US rules make a company name any customer that brings 10% or more of
   its revenue. From each universe company's original 10-Ks (EDGAR primary document, the same fetch and
   cleaning as M1), every sentence that mentions "customer" and a percentage and "revenue" or "sales" is
   kept. Capitalized name phrases in those sentences are matched to EDGAR company names of universe
   companies after normalizing (lower case, punctuation removed, the words inc, incorporated, corp,
   corporation, co, company, ltd, limited, plc, holdings, group, lp, llc and a leading "the" dropped). A
   match is an exact normalized match, or a single remaining word of at least 5 letters that matches
   exactly one universe company. No hand-made alias list. A company never links to itself. A link counts
   from the 10-K's filing date until the supplier's next 10-K or 15 months, whichever comes first.
2. **Shared-insider links (exploratory).** Two companies are linked when the same reporting person
   (an officer or director, not a 10%-owner-only filer) filed Forms 3/4/5 for both in the 24 months to t
   (SEC's DERA insider data sets, filing dates).
3. **News co-mention links (exploratory).** Two symbols are linked in month m when at least 3 Benzinga
   articles in the 3 months to m tag both of them and at most 4 symbols in total (bigger lists are market
   round-ups).

## Universe, dates, signal

- M3's month-end panel (`research/prereg/M3.md`, the M4 universe): the same 116 signal dates
  t = 2016-01-29 .. 2025-08-29, the same entry (next open) and 1-month exit, all before 2025-10-01.
- Signal for supplier i at t: the equal-weight mean of its linked customers' returns over the month
  ending at t (each customer's panel return from t-1 to t). Customers without that return are left
  out; a supplier with none isn't ranked.

## Primary test

Each t: rank the linked suppliers in the universe into quintiles by the signal; long the top quintile,
short the bottom, equal weight, held one month, with the main track's 0.2% round trip on each leg and
0.25% a month of short borrow (M1's cost rule). Dates with fewer than 50 ranked suppliers are skipped
and counted. Same-date random control: the same suppliers put into quintiles at random (numpy
default_rng(20261017 + date ordinal)).

**Pass** (PROTOCOL rules 11-12) only if the mean monthly long-short return > 0 with a Newey-West
t >= 3.0 (3 lags), the 95% block-bootstrap CI's lower bound > 0, and the Fama-French 5 + momentum
alpha > 0 with t >= 2.0. The random control is reported alongside. The deflated Sharpe ratio is reported with the IDEAS.md count as trials.

A fail ends G1 as a trading idea; the link card below still ships, as a reading aid with no claim
attached. A pass leads to a forward paper test registered separately.

## Data QA (PROTOCOL rule 7)

50 random customer links are written to `research/results/G1/G1_links_check.csv` and checked by hand
against the 10-K sentence before any return is computed. **If fewer than 40 of the 50 are right, the
screen doesn't run** and the extraction is fixed under a new registration. Coverage is reported per
year: linked suppliers per date, links per supplier, and how many 10-Ks had a customer sentence but no
match.

## Pipeline check (PROTOCOL rule 14)

G1 uses M3's panel, so it counts only if M3's pipeline replication passes.

## Exploratory, decided now

- Shared-insider momentum and co-mention momentum with the same quintile long-short.
- The reverse direction (suppliers' returns predicting customers).
- Weighting customers by the stated revenue share when the sentence gives one.

## The link card (a reading aid, not a test)

`python -m tracks.main.g1_links card --symbol XYZ` prints, for one stock as of today: its customers and
suppliers with the 10-K sentence and filing link, companies sharing an officer or director (with the
person as named in the public filing), the symbols most often in the same headlines, and each linked
company's last-month return and latest 8-K. No signal or score is shown on the card until the screen
has passed.
