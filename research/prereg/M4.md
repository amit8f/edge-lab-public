# M4: long-term multi-factor ranking (main track)

Registered 2026-10-12, before any return, rank or score of this idea was computed. Requested by Amit as
"a dashboard of the best stocks to hold for the coming months". Phase-2 count at registration: 1
(main 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 16.

## Question

Do the 20 highest-ranked stocks on an equal-weight composite of six published long-horizon factors beat
the equal-weight universe over the following 6 months, after costs?

## Universe (point-in-time, at each signal date t)

- Alpaca us_equity assets, active and inactive (delisted kept), classified as common stock by
  `core.instruments.classify` (no ADRs, funds, units, warrants, preferreds; listed exchanges only).
- Ticker -> SEC CIK as of t: the CIK of the latest Form 3/4/5 (SEC DERA insider data sets) filed on or
  before t that names the ticker, within 730 days. Unmapped tickers are dropped and counted.
- On t: raw close >= $5; market cap >= $300M (raw close x the latest cover-page shares outstanding filed
  on or before t, split-corrected to t); median 20-day raw dollar volume >= $2M; at least 253 bars in the
  current series (a >15-calendar-day gap ends a series; zero-volume bars dropped).
- One line per company per date (dual classes: keep the higher 20-day dollar volume).
- At least 4 of the 6 factor families present.

## Data

Alpaca SIP daily bars, `adjustment=all` for returns and price factors, `raw` for price, dollar volume
and market cap, `split` only to split-correct share counts. SEC companyfacts (XBRL), each fact usable
from its first filing date: annual 10-K values (original, not restated) for net income, gross profit
(or revenue minus cost of revenue), total assets, stockholders' equity; cover-page shares outstanding
from any 10-K or 10-Q. An annual record older than 540 days at t is not used.

## Signal (no fitted parameters)

Six families, each a cross-sectional percentile rank on t (higher = better):

1. Momentum: total return from t-252 to t-21 trading days.
2. Low volatility: minus the annualised std of daily log returns over 252 days.
3. Quality: mean of the ranks of gross profit / assets and net income / assets.
4. Value: mean of the ranks of earnings / market cap and book equity / market cap.
5. Net issuance: minus log growth of split-corrected shares outstanding over 12 months (undefined when a
   split happened within 6 months of either share date).
6. Asset growth: minus the 1-year growth of total assets.

Composite = mean of the available family ranks. The 20 highest composites are the picks.

## Entry, exit, costs

Signal dates: last trading day of each month, 2015-12-31 .. 2025-03-31 (112 dates). Entry at the next
trading day's open, exit at the close 126 trading days after t (adjusted prices). A series that ends
early (delisting or a >15-day gap) exits at its last close. Equal weight. Cost: the main track's
round-trip 0.2% from `config/risk.yaml`, charged once per pick. Every 6-month window ends before
2025-10-01, so no label touches the phase-2 holdout.

## Primary test

Per signal date: mean 6-month return of the 20 picks minus the mean 6-month return of the whole
eligible universe minus 0.2%. Statistic: the mean of that series over the 112 dates, Newey-West t-stat
with 5 lags (windows overlap by up to 5 months), and a moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 6
months, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261012).

**Screen passes** only if the mean > 0, the HAC t >= 2.0 and the CI's lower bound > 0. This screen can
only reject. A pass sends M4 to forward paper logging, which is the confirmatory test.

## Control

The same metric for 20 stocks drawn at random from the same day's eligible universe (numpy
default_rng(20261012 + date ordinal)). Expected to be ~0; a control that is clearly non-zero means a
data bug, and the run stops for QA.

## Exploratory, decided now (reported as exploratory, never as findings)

12-month horizon; top 50; each family alone; excess over SPY instead of the universe; per-year results;
composite deciles (monotonicity); a calibration table by composite percentile bucket (median 6-month
return, 25th/75th percentile, share beating SPY) used only to label the dashboard's numbers.

## Forward test (confirmatory)

From the first month-end after this file is merged, the nightly job logs each month-end's 20 picks and
a 20-stock random control into the hash-chained ledger. After 12 logged months (6-month outcomes for
the first 6 at least), the same primary metric on the forward picks is reported. No real money.

## Stop rule

If the screen fails, the dashboard may still show the ranking, but labelled "no measured edge" with the
backtest numbers next to it, and M4 is not paper traded.

## Data QA (equity-data-qa)

Funnel counts before any return; hand-check the 20 largest 6-month returns against raw bars; min/max and
percentiles of every return column; universe mean vs SPY; random control near zero.
