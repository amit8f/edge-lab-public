# M1: filing changes ("Lazy Prices", main track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any filing text, similarity or return of this idea was computed. From plan
v2 (`docs/PLAN_V2.md`). Phase-2 count at registration: 6 (main 6). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 21.

## Question

Do companies whose latest 10-K or 10-Q barely changed from the same report a year earlier beat
companies whose report changed the most, over the next month, after costs? (Cohen, Malloy & Nguyen,
"Lazy Prices", 2020.)

## Universe, dates

M4's universe (`research/prereg/M4.md`: common stock, CIK mapped as of t, raw close >= $5, market cap
>= $300M, median 20-day dollar volume >= $2M, 253 bars, one line per company), on each signal date t =
the last trading day of each month, 2016-01-29 .. 2025-08-29 (116 dates). Every 1-month window ends
before 2025-10-01.

## Data

SEC EDGAR, original filings only (no /A): 10-K and 10-Q primary documents of universe companies, filed
2014-12-01 .. 2025-08-29, each usable from its filing date. Text: HTML tags, tables of numbers
(table cells with no letters), page numbers and exhibits removed; lower-cased; tokens are runs of
letters of length 3 or more.

## Signal (no fitted parameters, no model)

For a filing F, its comparison is the same form type filed by the same CIK 300-430 days before F
(for a 10-Q, the one closest to 365 days before). Similarity = cosine similarity of the two
documents' term-count vectors. At t, a company's score is the similarity of its most recent 10-K or
10-Q filed in (t - 91 days, t] that has a comparison; companies without one are left out (counted).

## Portfolios, entry, exit, costs

At each t, sort the scored companies into deciles. Long the top decile (least changed), short the
bottom decile (most changed), equal weight. Entry at the next trading day's open, exit at the close
of the next month's last trading day (`adjustment=all`); a series that ends early exits at its last
close. Costs from `config/risk.yaml` (main): 0.2% round trip per name per month on both legs, as if
every name turned over, and borrow of 3% a year (0.25% a month) on the short leg.

## Primary test

Per t: long-decile mean return minus short-decile mean return minus costs. Statistic: the mean over
the 116 months, Newey-West t with 3 lags, and a moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 6 months,
10,000 resamples, seed 20261015).

**Screen passes** only if the mean > 0, the HAC t >= 2.0 and the CI's lower bound > 0. The screen can
only reject. A pass sends M1 to forward paper trading (below), which is the confirmatory test.

## Control

The same statistic with the two deciles drawn at random from the same month's scored companies
(numpy default_rng(20261015 + date ordinal)). A control clearly away from 0 means a data bug; the run
stops for QA.

## Exploratory, decided now (never reported as findings)

Long leg minus the scored set (long-only version); quintiles; 3-month hold; 10-K only and 10-Q only;
the risk-factor section (Item 1A) alone; Jaccard similarity; per year; the share of companies scored
per date. A local embedding model or an LLM reading the changes is a new idea with its own
registration, compared against this one as the baseline.

## Forward test (confirmatory, only after a screen pass)

From the first month-end after the screen passes: the nightly job scores filings as they arrive and
logs the two deciles each month-end into the hash-chained ledger; the paper book trades at most 10
positions (5 long, 5 short: the extreme names of each decile) under the main track's risk rules, with
a month-end time exit. After 12 logged months the primary statistic is computed on the forward
deciles. No real money.

## Stop rule

A failed screen ends M1. The result is logged with the running count.

## Data QA (equity-data-qa)

Funnel counts (filings found, parsed, with a comparison, scored per date); 20 random filing pairs
checked by hand (right company, right prior-year report, text not empty); similarity percentiles by
year; universe mean return vs SPY; the random control near zero.

## Clarifications (2026-10-15, before any filing was downloaded or any similarity computed)

1. Universe rows, dates and forward returns are the M3 screen's month-end rows (`tracks/main/m3_screen.py`,
   same 116 dates, same M4 eligibility rules, same entry and exit), so both screens share one bar download.
2. Filings come from EDGAR's submissions API (`primaryDocument` of each filing whose form is exactly
   `10-K` or `10-Q`). A filing whose document can't be fetched is counted as not parsed.
3. A 10-K's comparison, like a 10-Q's, is the one closest to 365 days before; on a tie, the earlier one.
4. Cleaning: the hidden inline-XBRL header, scripts and styles are dropped; a table cell is dropped when
   its text has no letter; a page-number line is a line holding only a number or roman numeral (with an
   optional "page", dashes or "of n"). Exhibits are excluded by using the primary document only.
5. Costs per month: long leg 0.2%, short leg 0.2% + 0.25% borrow, so the long-short spread pays 0.65%
   The exploratory 3-month hold pays the round trip once and borrow for 3 months: 2 x 0.2% + 0.75% = 1.15%. Deciles are by rank (ties broken by order), `pd.qcut` into 10.
6. Item 1A (exploratory) is the longest span from an "Item 1A. Risk Factors" heading to the next
   "Item 1B" or "Item 2".
7. The 20 random pairs for the hand check are written to `research/results/M1/M1_pairs_check.csv`; Claude
   checks them and commits the result before the screen is reported.
