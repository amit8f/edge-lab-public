# B0: what was visible before a stock jumped 30% in two weeks? (penny and main, base rates)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any of this idea's data was downloaded. Amit's "burst" request, as
rewritten in the deeper plan. Phase-2 count at registration: 15 (main 9, penny 3, news 2, cross-track 1).
Phase 1 + phase 2 = 30.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- **Counterparty:** in a squeeze, short sellers forced to buy back; in a re-rating, sellers who missed
  public news (a filing, a contract, a trial result). Both leave traces before the jump: high short
  interest and fails-to-deliver, or a filing in the record.
- **Why us:** most of these stocks are below $2B and thinly traded, too small for funds to hold
  meaningful positions.
- **Decay:** short-squeeze predictors are studied (days-to-cover, Hong et al. 2016), mostly in larger
  stocks. Nothing here is assumed to work.

## Events and matched controls

- Universe on day t: common stock, raw close >= $1, median 20-day raw dollar volume >= $500k; penny
  ($1-5) and main ($5+) are reported separately.
- Burst: the highest close over the next 10 trading days is >= 1.30 x the close on t (`adjustment=all`),
  in the same series. Only the first burst day per stock in any 20 trading days counts.
- Controls: for each burst, 5 stocks from the same day with no burst, matched on price group, dollar
  volume quintile and 60-day volatility decile (default_rng(20261015 + date ordinal)).
- Development period: t in 2016-01-04 .. 2022-12-30. Test period: t in 2023-01-03 .. 2025-09-15 (every
  10-day window ends before 2025-10-01).

## Features (all as known at t's close, each from its publication date)

1. Days to cover: FINRA short interest / 20-day average volume. Only the latest file published by t
   counts; files publish about 7 business days after the settlement date.
2. Reg SHO short volume ratio: FINRA daily short volume over total volume, 5-day mean.
3. Fails-to-deliver: SEC FTD files. Only a half-month file published by t counts; the mean fails over
   the period is scaled by shares outstanding.
4. Float pressure: an S-1 or 424B4 IPO filed 150-200 days before t (lockup expiry window).
5. Insider buys: officer or director code-P purchases in the 90 days to t (Form 4 filed date).
6. Recent filings: the count of 8-Ks in the 20 days to t, and whether an S-1, S-3 or 424B was filed in
   the 90 days to t.
7. Price setup: the 20-day return, the 60-day return, and the distance from the 52-week high.
8. News: the count of Benzinga articles in the 5 days to t.

Options features (call/put volume ratio, implied-volatility skew) exist only from 2024-02 on Alpaca.
They are exploratory, on the test period only.

## Primary test

On the development period, fit one L2 logistic regression per price group: burst vs matched controls,
on the standardized features above, with the penalty fixed at C = 1.0 and no tuning. On the test
period, report the AUC with a bootstrap 95% CI (resampling dates, 2,000 times, seed 20261015).

**A pass** (per price group) needs a test AUC >= 0.60 with the CI's lower bound >= 0.55.

Even at a pass, a burst model is only useful if the top-scored stocks don't crash as often as they
jump. So it also reports, for the test period's top-decile scores in the full universe (not just
matched rows): the probability of +30% within 10 days, the probability of -20% within 10 days, and
the mean 10-day return after costs. Each comes with its CI and the same numbers for the full universe
(the base rate).

A pass leads to a separate pre-registration of a trading rule (B1) with a forward test. B0 trades
nothing.

## Exploratory, decided now

- Each feature alone (univariate test AUC).
- Options features on 2024-2025.
- Burst thresholds of +20% and +50%.
- 5-day windows.

## Data QA

20 bursts checked by hand against raw bars (no split or reverse-split artifact). Check the publication
lag of each FINRA and FTD file used. Report feature coverage per year.

## Stop rule

A fail in both price groups ends B0. Burst trading stays out of the paper book unless a later,
separately registered idea passes.
