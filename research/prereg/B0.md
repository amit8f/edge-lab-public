# B0: what was visible before a stock jumped 30% in two weeks? (penny and main, base rates)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any of this idea's data was downloaded. Amit's "burst" request, as
rewritten in the deeper plan. Phase-2 count at registration: 15 (main 9, penny 3, news 2, cross-track 1).
Phase 1 + phase 2 = 30.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- **Counterparty:** in a squeeze, short sellers forced to buy back; in a re-rating, sellers who missed
  public news (a filing, a contract, a trial result). Both leave traces before the jump: high short
  interest and fails-to-deliver, or a filing in the record.
- **Why us:** most of these stocks are below $2B and thinly traded, too small for funds to hold
  meaningful positions.
- **Decay:** short-squeeze predictors are studied (days-to-cover, Hong et al. 2016), mostly in larger
  stocks. Nothing here is assumed to work.

## Events and matched controls

- Universe on day t: common stock, raw close >= $1, median 20-day raw dollar volume >= $500k; penny
  ($1-5) and main ($5+) are reported separately.
- Burst: the highest close over the next 10 trading days is >= 1.30 x the close on t (`adjustment=all`),
  in the same series. Only the first burst day per stock in any 20 trading days counts.
- Controls: for each burst, 5 stocks from the same day with no burst, matched on price group, dollar
  volume quintile and 60-day volatility decile (default_rng(20261015 + date ordinal)).
- Development period: t in 2016-01-04 .. 2022-12-30. Test period: t in 2023-01-03 .. 2025-09-15 (every
  10-day window ends before 2025-10-01).

## Features (all as known at t's close, each from its publication date)

1. Days to cover: FINRA short interest / 20-day average volume. Only the latest file published by t
   counts; files publish about 7 business days after the settlement date.
2. Reg SHO short volume ratio: FINRA daily short volume over total volume, 5-day mean.
3. Fails-to-deliver: SEC FTD files. Only a half-month file published by t counts; the mean fails over
   the period is scaled by shares outstanding.
4. Float pressure: an S-1 or 424B4 IPO filed 150-200 days before t (lockup expiry window).
5. Insider buys: officer or director code-P purchases in the 90 days to t (Form 4 filed date).
6. Recent filings: the count of 8-Ks in the 20 days to t, and whether an S-1, S-3 or 424B was filed in
   the 90 days to t.
7. Price setup: the 20-day return, the 60-day return, and the distance from the 52-week high.
8. News: the count of Benzinga articles in the 5 days to t.

Options features (call/put volume ratio, implied-volatility skew) exist only from 2024-02 on Alpaca.
They are exploratory, on the test period only.

## Primary test

On the development period, fit one L2 logistic regression per price group: burst vs matched controls,
on the standardized features above, with the penalty fixed at C = 1.0 and no tuning. On the test
period, report the AUC with a bootstrap 95% CI (resampling dates, 2,000 times, seed 20261015).

**A pass** (per price group) needs a test AUC >= 0.60 with the CI's lower bound >= 0.55.

Even at a pass, a burst model is only useful if the top-scored stocks don't crash as often as they
jump. So it also reports, for the test period's top-decile scores in the full universe (not just
matched rows): the probability of +30% within 10 days, the probability of -20% within 10 days, and
the mean 10-day return after costs. Each comes with its CI and the same numbers for the full universe
(the base rate).

A pass leads to a separate pre-registration of a trading rule (B1) with a forward test. B0 trades
nothing.

## Exploratory, decided now

- Each feature alone (univariate test AUC).
- Options features on 2024-2025.
- Burst thresholds of +20% and +50%.
- 5-day windows.

## Data QA

20 bursts checked by hand against raw bars (no split or reverse-split artifact). Check the publication
lag of each FINRA and FTD file used. Report feature coverage per year.

## Stop rule

A fail in both price groups ends B0. Burst trading stays out of the paper book unless a later,
separately registered idea passes.

## Clarifications (2026-10-15, before any of B0's data was downloaded)

The code is `tracks/penny/b0_study.py` with `core/shortdata.py` (workflow `b0-study`, manual runs only).
The FINRA and SEC file formats were confirmed from GitHub's servers by `core/probe.py` the same day; no
B0 rows were read.

1. Bars: Alpaca SIP daily bars from 2014-12-01 to 2025-09-30, raw and `adjustment=all`, delisted stocks
   kept, common stock only (`core.instruments` classification, as in T1). Dollar volume needs 20 bars and
   volatility 60 daily log returns in the same series. Price group uses the raw close on t.
2. Burst window: the next 10 (or 5) trading days of the exchange calendar after t, using daily closes, in
   the same series. A stock that stops trading inside the window is measured on the bars it has. The
   20-day spacing counts trading days from the last counted burst. Burst days that aren't counted are
   neither events nor controls.
3. Matching: quintiles and deciles are computed per date within each price group over all universe rows.
   Bursts are matched in symbol order, controls drawn without replacement within the day; a burst whose
   cell runs short gets fewer than 5 (the count is reported). Each variant draws with a fresh
   default_rng(20261015 + date ordinal).
4. Days to cover: FINRA's consolidated short interest (public API, no key). A settlement counts from the
   8th trading day after the settlement date (the registered ~7 plus one day of margin), and only while
   it is at most 45 calendar days old at t. It is divided by the mean raw volume of the 20 bars to t, and
   left missing when the raw-to-adjusted price ratio jumped by more than 20% in the 30 bars to t (a split
   between settlement and t would mix share bases).
5. Short volume ratio: FINRA's consolidated daily file (CNMS) for each day, or where it doesn't exist the
   sum of the facility files (FNSQ, FNQC, FNYX, FNRA). These cover off-exchange volume only, so the ratio
   is the feature. The 5-day mean over the 5 trading days to t needs at least 3 of them.
6. Fails-to-deliver: a half-month file counts from the first trading day 30 calendar days after the end of
   its period, and only while that period ended at most 75 days before t. Mean fails = the symbol's total
   fails in the file / the number of settlement days in it; a posted file that doesn't list the symbol
   means zero. Scaled in basis points of the latest cover-page shares outstanding
   (`dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding`, companyfacts) filed by t, within 400 days. Each file's
   Last-Modified header is logged, and files posted later than the assumed date are listed in the report.
7. SEC filings come from EDGAR's quarterly form index (filing dates), mapped to symbols through the DERA
   insider data sets' (symbol, CIK, date) map (latest within 730 days), as in M4.
   - Float pressure: a 424B4 filed 150-200 days before t when the symbol's first bar falls between 5 days
     before and 30 days after that 424B4, i.e. it was the IPO. An S-1 alone isn't an IPO, so it isn't used
     for this feature.
   - 8-Ks: original 8-Ks (no 8-K/A) filed in the 20 calendar days to t.
   - Offerings: S-1, S-3, 424B1-424B5, 424B7 or 424B8 (originals) filed in the 90 calendar days to t.
   - Insider buys: 1 if any officer or director code-P purchase on an original Form 4 was filed in the 90
     calendar days to t (the DERA parser M4 uses).
   - Rows without a CIK have these four missing.
8. News: Benzinga articles tagging the symbol, created after the close 5 trading days before t and up to
   t's close (early closes respected), from Alpaca's news history month by month.
9. Model inputs: days to cover, the 8-K count and the news count enter as log(1 + x). Every non-binary
   feature is clipped at its development-period 1st and 99th percentiles, then standardized with the
   development mean and standard deviation, all per price group. Missing values become 0 after
   standardizing (the development mean); there are no missing-value flags. scikit-learn's
   LogisticRegression(C=1.0), L2, lbfgs, no class weights.
10. The date bootstrap resamples test dates with replacement; all of a date's rows come along.
11. Top decile: within each test date and price group, the highest-scored 10% of universe rows (at least
    one). The 10-day return runs from the next day's adjusted open to the 10th trading day's adjusted
    close (the last close if the stock stops trading) minus the track's round trip in `config/risk.yaml`
    (penny 2%, main 0.2%). "-20% within 10 days" means the lowest close in the window is at most 0.8x t's
    close.
12. Exploratory variants (+20%, +50%, 5-day) get their own first-burst flags, matched controls, fit and
    test AUC. Options features aren't computed by this code; they would need a separate pull and stay
    exploratory if added later.
13. Hand check: 20 random primary bursts go to `research/results/B0/B0_bursts_check.csv` for checking
    against raw bars.
