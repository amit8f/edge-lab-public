# K3: Kronos weekly picks, forward test only (main track)

Registered 2026-10-16, before any Kronos forecast was made or any return existed. Amit sent
https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos ("should we add and use this?") and chose "Forward test" on
2026-10-16. Phase-2 count at registration: 21 (main 13, penny 4, news 2, cross-track 1, crypto 1).
Phase 1 + phase 2 = 36.

No real money. Picks are logged, never traded, and never enter the family plan or the dashboard's
buy list unless K3 passes.

## Why forward only

Kronos (Shi et al., 2025, MIT licence) is a transformer pre-trained on candlestick data from many
exchanges. Its training data and cutoff aren't fully documented and very likely include US stocks
through 2024 or later, so any historical screen on our data could be memorised. Following the rule for
LLM features (CLAUDE.md), only forecasts made after this registration count.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

1. **Counterparty.** If a pattern model reading two years of daily bars can rank one-month returns,
   it takes money from traders who react slowly to the same public price and volume history.
2. **Why a $1B fund can't take it away.** Nothing stops them: the model is public and the stocks are
   the 500 most traded. Any edge would be competed away fast.
3. **Published?** The Kronos paper reports forecasting accuracy and a backtest on Chinese A-shares,
   not out-of-sample US stock ranking. Price-only one-month ranking is mostly short-term reversal and
   momentum, both well known.

**Prediction written now:** K3 fails. Its picks behave like short-term reversal or momentum tilts and
don't beat the universe after costs.

## Model (pinned)

- Code: `shiyu-coder/Kronos` at commit `67b630e67f6a18c9e9be918d9b4337c960db1e9a`.
- Model `NeoQuasar/Kronos-small` (24.7M parameters, context 512) with tokenizer
  `NeoQuasar/Kronos-Tokenizer-base`, CPU, from HuggingFace. The first run resolves each repo's commit
  hash, logs it and stores it in the state branch; every later run loads exactly those hashes. If a
  pinned hash can't be loaded, that week is logged as missed.
- Settings: lookback 256 daily bars, `pred_len` 21, temperature 1.0, top_p 0.9, top_k 0,
  `sample_count` 4 (averaged inside Kronos), `clip` 5, torch seed 20261016 + date ordinal.
- Input per stock: open, high, low, close, volume from Alpaca SIP daily bars with `adjustment=all`
  (as known at the signal date). The forecast is `predicted close on step 21 / last close - 1`.

## Universe and timing

- Signal date t: the last trading session of each week (the next session falls in a later week), from
  the first such session after this registration reaches `main`. The job runs Saturday 03:30 UTC
  (after 10pm ET Friday) and logs nothing on other days.
- Candidates: Alpaca US-equity assets that `core.instruments.classify` keeps as common stock, listed
  on an exchange.
- Daily SIP bars with `adjustment=raw` for the 20 sessions up to t, fetched in ~500-symbol batches.
  Kept: last raw close >= $5 and median raw dollar volume (close x volume) over those 20 sessions;
  the 500 highest are the universe. Market cap isn't checked directly; at the 500th most traded name
  (tens of millions of dollars a day) every stock is far above $300M. This is disclosed, not tested.
- Each universe stock needs 256 clean `all`-adjusted daily bars ending at t: zero-volume bars dropped,
  and a gap of more than 15 calendar days ends the series (only bars after the last such gap count).
  Stocks without 256 bars are dropped and listed in the log.
- Logged (hash-chained ledger, branch `state-kronos`, `kronos/ledger.jsonl`, kind `K3_picks`): the top 20
  by forecast with their forecast and raw close, a random 20 from the same universe
  (`numpy.random.default_rng(20261016 + t.toordinal())`), every universe stock's forecast, the dropped
  list, the model hashes and the run time.

## Primary test

Per logged week: mean return of the 20 picks from the next session's open to the close of the 21st
session counting that one (adjustment=all), minus the universe mean over the same window, minus 0.2%
round-trip cost (`config/risk.yaml`, main). A stock whose series ends early keeps its last close
(disclosed; delistings are not dropped).

- First read at 26 logged weeks with outcomes (about May 2027), final at 52 (about November 2027).
- Statistic: mean, Newey-West t with 6 lags (weekly windows overlap about 4 weeks), moving-block
  bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 8, 10,000 draws, seed 20261016).
- **Passes** only if mean > 0, t >= 3.0 (PROTOCOL rule 12), the CI's lower bound > 0, and the
  FF5 + momentum alpha of the monthly-averaged excess return has t >= 2 (rule 11, 3 lags).
- A fail at 26 weeks is "not yet shown"; the verdict is at 52.

**Power, stated now.** With 52 overlapping weekly windows, a true edge of +1% a month after costs gives
an expected t of roughly 1 to 1.5 (about 13 independent months, monthly spread of 3-4%). A pass needs a very large edge; a fail mostly says "no large edge".

## Control and exploratory (never reported as findings)

- The random 20 against the universe (should be about zero; a clearly non-zero control stops the
  test for data QA).
- Rank correlation of forecast with realised 21-day return across the whole universe each week;
  bottom-20 picks; correlation of the forecast with 1-month reversal and 12-1 momentum; hit rate;
  share of picks falling 30% or more.

## Stop rule

A fail at 52 weeks ends K3. Four missed weeks in a row (model or data failures) stop the test and are
reported. A pass is a hypothesis for a new pre-registered test, never a reason to trade real money.

## Amendment 2026-10-16, 17:00 UTC (data QA, before any pick was logged)

A dry run on the 2026-10-15 close (nothing logged; 65 minutes, 492 forecasts) put CTVA first at +484% in
21 days, with the next names at +30% to +93%. A forecast that size points at a broken input series (for
example an unadjusted spin-off), not at the model. Rule added before the first logged week: a stock whose
256 input bars contain a one-day close change of -50% or worse, or +100% or more, is dropped and listed
with the other drops. Everything else is unchanged. The dry run's picks are not results and are not used.
