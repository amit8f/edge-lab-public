# M8: forward (paper) test of M6's analyst-action ranking (main track)

Registered 2026-10-12, before any forward pick of this idea was logged. Requested by Amit, who chose to
start a live paper test after M6 narrowly failed its historical screen (+3.0% per 6 months, t 1.61;
`research/results/M6/REPORT.md`). Phase-2 count at registration: 5 (main 5). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 20.

**Disclosure.** This idea exists because M6's historical result looked promising. That makes it
post-hoc; only data after this registration counts, and the historical numbers are not evidence for
it.

## Rule (unchanged from M6)

Classification rules, 90-day window, score, tie-breaks and universe are exactly those of
`research/prereg/M6.md`, applied to Benzinga headlines from Alpaca's news API created in the 91 days up
to the signal date's close, and to the nightly job's live universe (M4's eligibility filters: $5+,
$300M+, $2M median dollar volume, a CIK in SEC's current ticker list, delisted names kept once logged).

## Logging

On the last trading day of each month, from 2026-10-30 on, the nightly job writes to the hash-chained
ledger (`state/longterm/ledger.jsonl`, kind `M8_picks`): the top 20 with score and raw close, a 20-stock
random control from the same universe (numpy default_rng(20261012 + date ordinal)), and the full
universe. No real money.

## Primary test (confirmatory)

Per logged month-end: mean return of the 20 picks from the next open to the close 126 trading days
later, minus the universe mean, minus 0.2%. Reported first when 12 month-ends have 6-month outcomes
(about April 2028), then yearly. Statistic: mean, Newey-West t with 5 lags, moving-block bootstrap 95% CI
(blocks of 6, 10,000 draws, seed 20261012). **Passes** only if mean > 0, t >= 2.0 and the CI's lower
bound > 0. Twelve overlapping windows give low power; a fail at 12 is reported as "not yet shown", and
the final verdict is at 24 month-ends.

## Dashboard

M8's latest logged picks are shown in their own section labelled "under live test", never as Buy, and
never merged into the M4 ranking unless M8 passes.

## Stop rule

A fail at 24 month-ends ends M8. A random control whose mean is clearly non-zero stops the test for QA.

## Amendment 2026-10-15 (before any M8 entry was logged; replaces Logging, Primary test and Stop rule)

Amit asked for a shorter test and chose "weekly, 1-month hold" on 2026-10-15. No M8 pick had been
logged (the ledger had no `M8_picks` entry), so this changes the plan, not a result.

- **Logging.** On the last trading day of each week (the next trading day falls in a later week),
  from the first such day after this amendment reaches `main`, the nightly job logs kind `M8_picks`
  exactly as above: top 20 by M6's rules on the 91 days of headlines up to that close, the same-seed
  random 20, and the universe.
- **Primary test.** Per logged week: mean return of the 20 picks from the next open to the close 21
  trading days later, minus the universe mean, minus 0.2%. First reported when 26 weeks have
  1-month outcomes (about May 2027), final at 52 weeks. Newey-West t with 6 lags (weekly windows
  overlap about 4 weeks), moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 8, 10,000 draws, seed 20261012).
  **Passes** only if mean > 0, t >= 2.0 and the CI's lower bound > 0. A fail at 26 is reported as
  "not yet shown".
- **Disclosure.** M6 was screened at 6 months, not 1 month; the 1-month horizon has no historical
  screen behind it. A historical check of M6's ranking at 21 days on pre-2025-10-01 weekly data is
  run after this amendment and reported as post-hoc context only; it cannot change this plan.
- **Stop rule.** A fail at 52 weeks ends M8. A random control whose mean is clearly non-zero stops
  the test for QA.
