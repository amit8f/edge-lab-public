# S1: 5-minute VWAP snap-back scalp, historical screen on stocks and crypto (cross-track)

Registered 2026-10-17, before any data for this idea was downloaded or any result computed. Source: Amit
sent Alex Carter's video "I Built a 5-Minute Scalping Bot Using Claude Code" (YouTube 4iEKwDmbp9c,
published 2026-10-17) and chose "Stocks and crypto" on the test card. The video gives entry rules only,
no exits and no results. Phase-2 count at registration: 23 (main 14, penny 4, news 2, cross-track 2,
crypto 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 38.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

1. **Counterparty.** Short-term traders who push price far from the day's volume-weighted average on a
   burst of volume (stop runs, liquidations, news overreaction) and pay for immediacy; the scalper is
   the liquidity provider who gets paid when price snaps back.
2. **Why a $1B fund can't take it away.** It can, and market makers and HFT firms do exactly this at
   sub-second speed with rebates instead of fees. Whatever survives at 5-minute speed after retail
   costs is expected to be small.
3. **Published?** Intraday reversal after large moves is documented (e.g. Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka
   2010 on intraday periodicity; liquidity-provision returns in Nagel 2012), and mostly sits inside
   the bid-ask spread. VWAP bands plus Stochastic RSI plus relative volume is a retail indicator combo
   with no published test.

**Prediction written now:** both markets fail after costs; gross returns may be slightly positive.

## Data and universe

- **Stocks.** For each calendar year Y from 2018 to 2025: SPY, QQQ and the 20 listed common stocks
  (`core.instruments.classify`, active and inactive Alpaca assets, so delisted names count) with the
  highest median daily raw dollar volume in year Y-1 among those closing at $5 or more on the last
  session of Y-1. Alpaca SIP 5-minute bars, `adjustment=all` (returns), regular session only
  (9:30-16:00 ET, Alpaca calendar), zero-volume bars dropped. Period 2018-01-02 to 2025-09-30; the
  phase-2 holdout from 2025-10-01 is not touched.
- **Crypto.** BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT spot 5-minute klines from Binance's public archive
  (data.binance.vision, free, no key; the video trades BTCUSDT), 2021-01-01 to 2025-09-30 UTC. If the
  archive is unreachable from the runner, Alpaca's BTC/USD and ETH/USD 5-minute bars are used instead
  and the report says so. A crypto "session" is the UTC day (TradingView's VWAP anchor for crypto).

## Indicators (the video's settings)

- **VWAP** anchored at each session start, on typical price (high+low+close)/3, with bands at
  VWAP +/- k x the volume-weighted standard deviation of typical price since the anchor; k = 1 and 2.
- **Stochastic RSI** (RSI 14 Wilder on close, stochastic length 14, K smoothing 3, D smoothing 3).
- **Relative volume** = bar volume / mean volume of the previous 20 bars (within the series, across
  sessions).

## Signal, entry, exit

- **Long** when a bar closes below the lower 2-sigma band ("outside the bands"), StochRSI K < 20 and
  rising (K > previous K), relative volume > 1.5 and the bar is green (close > open).
- **Short** is the mirror: close above the upper 2-sigma band, K > 80 and falling, relative volume >
  1.5, red bar.
- Signals need at least 20 bars since the session anchor (bands defined) and a full indicator history.
- **Entry** at the next bar's open. **Exit** (fixed here because the video gives none): at the close of
  the first bar after entry whose close reaches VWAP (long: close >= VWAP; short: close <= VWAP), else at
  the close of the 12th bar after entry (one hour), else at the session's last bar (stocks; crypto
  sessions roll at UTC midnight, and a crypto trade still open then exits at that bar). One position per
  symbol at a time; no new signal while one is open.

## Costs (config/risk.yaml)

- Stocks: main track round trip 0.2% of notional; shorts allowed only if Alpaca lists the stock as
  easy-to-borrow today (disclosed limitation: no point-in-time borrow list; all names in this universe
  are mega-caps) and borrow is not charged for trades under one hour.
- Crypto: the crypto block's 0.05% taker fee per side (0.1% round trip; the video trades futures);
  funding is not charged for trades under one hour.

## Control

Each signal trade gets one random twin: same symbol, same session, same side, entry at a bar drawn
uniformly from the session's eligible bars (same rule for bands defined; at least one bar left), same
exit rule and costs (seed 20261017).

## Primary test (one per market, each judged on its own)

Daily series: for each date with at least one signal trade, mean net return of that date's signal trades
minus the mean net return of their control twins. Pass (PROTOCOL rules 11-12, `core.stats.screen_pass`):
mean > 0, Newey-West t (5 lags) >= 3.0, moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 20 days, 10,000 draws)
above 0, and for stocks the monthly sum of the daily signal-trade mean net returns has Fama-French
5 + momentum alpha > 0 with t >= 2 (Newey-West, 3 lags). Crypto has no equity factor test (disclosed).
Fewer than 200 signal trades in a market: "no conclusion" for that market. The deflated Sharpe ratio
of the daily net series uses the phase 1 + phase 2 count (38) as trials.

A pass on this screen can only send the rule to a forward paper test under its own pre-registration.

## Pipeline check (PROTOCOL rule 14, substitute disclosed)

No published intraday result is replicated. Instead, before results: for SPY, the last regular-session
5-minute close (the last trade before 16:00, so not the closing auction) must be within 0.25% of the
daily `adjustment=all` close on at least 99% of sessions, and summed regular-session 5-minute volume must
be 60% to 105% of daily volume on at least 95% (the daily bar also counts the closing auction and
extended-hours prints). If it fails, the stock screen stops.

## Exploratory (never reported as findings)

1-sigma bands; gross returns before costs; long vs short; per-symbol and per-year results; stock costs of
0.02% round trip (closer to SPY/QQQ's real spread); crypto at Alpaca's 0.25% per side; exits at 6 and 24
bars; hit rate; average hold.
