# G2: linked companies with a stricter customer matcher (main track)

Registered 2026-10-24, before any G1 or G2 return was computed (G1 stopped at its link hand check). Amit's
decision in the failure review grill (`research/FAILURE_REVIEW_2026-10-24.md`). Phase-2 count at
registration: 49 (main 24). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 64.

## Why

G1 (`prereg/G1.md`) never tested its idea, which comes from Cohen and Frazzini (2008): a customer's news
reaches its supplier's price late. Its link hand check found only 15 of 50 links right
(`research/results/G1/G1_links_check.csv`). The misses had three causes:
1. Single words matched as the first part of a company name ("Rocky" to Rocky Brands, "Advantage" to
   Conyers Park, "Canadian" to Canadian Pacific).
2. Sentences that aren't about customers ("our Industrial segment represents 47% of sales").
3. Percentages under the 10% that makes a customer reportable.

These fixes come from the hand check, not from any return, so G2's historical test is still clean: no
G1 or G2 return exists yet.

## Changes to G1 (everything else as G1, including universe, signal, test, pass bar and holdout)

1. **Exact names only.** A candidate phrase matches a universe company only when its normalised string
   (G1's normalising) equals that company's normalised name exactly. No first-part (prefix) matches.
   A one-word match also needs the word to be at least 5 letters long.
2. **Customer sentences only.** A kept sentence must contain one of: customer, customers, distributor,
   distributors, wholesaler, wholesalers, retailer, retailers, or "sales to". Sentences that also
   contain segment, segments, tenant, tenants, region or geographic are dropped.
3. **Reportable share.** The sentence's largest percentage must be at least 10%.
4. A company never links to itself (as G1).

## Data QA first

50 new random customer links (numpy default_rng(20261024)) are checked by hand against the 10-K sentence
and written to `research/results/G2/G2_links_check.csv` before any return is computed. **Fewer than 40
right stops G2.** Coverage is reported per year next to G1's (G2 will have fewer links, and the screen
needs >= 50 ranked suppliers per date; dates with fewer are skipped and counted, as G1).

## Primary test and pass

G1's, unchanged: monthly long-short quintile return of linked suppliers by their customers' past-month
return, Newey-West t >= 3.0, CI above 0, FF5 + momentum alpha t >= 2.0, deflated Sharpe with the
running count as trials, the same-date random control alongside. A pass gets a forward shadow book.

Prediction: about 70% fail (stricter matching leaves fewer suppliers; the published effect is from
1980-2004 and may have faded like M1's).
