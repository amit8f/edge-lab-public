# K4: an AI stock trader with $100 that picks from the whole liquid market, paper only (main track)

Registered 2026-10-16, before the bot made any decision or any return existed. Amit asked why the $100
bots only see ten stocks and hold one at a time ("why dont you let them choose from the entire stock
market which stock to buy (long) and which to short? ... why it cant hold AMZN and TSLA?") and chose to
replace K2's book B with this bot ("Replace B with C", 2026-10-16 19:02 UTC). Phase-2 count at
registration: 22 (main 14, penny 4, news 2, cross-track 1, crypto 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 37.

No real money. Fills are simulated in our own ledger from Alpaca SIP prices; a copy in an Alpaca paper
account is display only.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

1. **Counterparty.** If a language model reading the day's biggest movers, their recent hourly closes
   and their headlines can call the next hours' direction, it takes money from short-term traders
   reacting to the same public moves and news.
2. **Why a $1B fund can't take it away.** Nothing stops them: these are the 300 most traded stocks.
3. **Published?** Lopez-Lira & Tang (2023): LLM reading of headlines predicted next-day returns, mostly
   in small stocks and fading after publication. Intraday reversal and continuation after big moves are
   well studied and small after costs.

**Prediction written now:** C shows no skill against random sides on its own trades and trails SPY.

## Universe, data, timing

- Each trading day, before its first decision: Alpaca's active US common stocks (`core.instruments.classify`),
  ranked by median raw dollar volume over the 20 sessions before today; the top 300 with a last close of
  $5 or more. At that size all are far above the main track's $300M cap floor. Logged as
  `wide/universe/{date}.json`, with the set Alpaca marks shortable and easy to borrow.
- Each hour, the same schedule as K2 (minute 7, decisions only when now - 16 min is inside the regular
  session): the last 1-minute SIP close (`adjustment=raw`) at or before now - 16 min for every universe
  stock, SPY and anything held. The 40 universe stocks with the largest absolute move from the previous
  close, plus holdings, form the shortlist.
- The model sees for each shortlisted stock: last price, % since the previous close, % over 5 and 20
  sessions, shortable or not, the last 7 regular-session hourly closes, and up to 3 Alpaca news headlines
  published in the 24 hours before the price time. Headlines are not stored in the public log (license);
  their count per stock is.

## Books (all start with $100)

| Book | What it is |
|---|---|
| C | Gemini 2.5 Flash (free key, temperature 0) names the full set of up to 5 positions it wants, long or short, each with one line of reasoning. Positions left out are closed; new ones are opened at one fifth of equity; kept ones are not resized. Symbols outside the shortlist, shorts of names not shortable and anything past five are dropped and logged. An unreadable or failed answer changes nothing. |
| C-coin | The same symbols at the same moments, side drawn at random (seed 20261019); closes when C closes. |
| HOLD | Buys $100 of SPY on the first run and never trades. |

Gemini 2.5 Flash's training cutoff (2025-01-31) is before every date in the test.

## Costs (main track, `config/risk.yaml` `ai_trader`)

0.1% of notional per side; shorts pay 3% a year borrow on notional. No margin: gross exposure starts at
1x and drifts with prices. A held stock without a trade in the last hour keeps its last price.

## Primary test (2027-04-19, six months after the first run)

Replay C's logged round trips 10,000 times with each trip's side drawn at random (seed 20261019),
keeping each trip's logged notional, entry and exit prices, fees and borrow; total dollar P&L of each
replay against C's actual total. p = share of replays at or above C's actual. **Skill is shown** only if C
made at least 30 round trips and p < 0.05. Fewer than 30 round trips: "no conclusion".

## Secondary and exploratory (never reported as findings)

C against C-coin, HOLD and K2's book A; maximum drawdown; win rate; average hold; number of positions
held; long vs short trips; trips in stocks with vs without headlines.

## Stop rule

C below $5 stops trading and its result is final. A pass is a hypothesis for a new pre-registered test,
never a reason to trade real money (that needs the owner's written change to PROTOCOL.md).

## Display copy

After each run, C's positions are copied at 25x into the $2,500 Alpaca paper account that K2's book B
used (`tracks/main/ai_mirror.py`): longs fractional, shorts rounded to whole shares. Orders and fills go
to `wide/mirror.jsonl`. Nothing above uses the copy.

## Amendment 2026-10-16, 19:10 UTC (before C's first decision): real-time IEX prices

Amit approved ("ok do it") using Alpaca's free real-time IEX feed for C, an owner-approved exception to the
"always `feed=sip`" rule for this bot's current prices only. Each run, a stock's current price (shortlist
ranking, what the model sees as last price, and fills) is the latest IEX trade when it is inside the
regular session and newer than the last SIP 1-minute close at now - 16 minutes; otherwise the SIP close as
above. The decision time is then the run time. Hourly bars, the daily universe and every other input stay
SIP. Each run logs which source priced each shortlisted stock. IEX is one exchange (about 2-3% of US
volume), so its last trade can differ slightly from the consolidated price; this makes the simulated fills
closer to the Alpaca paper fills. The primary test is unchanged.
