## What the literature predicts (written before looking)

Stocks that look like lottery tickets have earned lower returns than other stocks. That covers high
recent maximum daily return, high idiosyncratic volatility and high expected skewness:

- Bali, Cakici and Whitelaw (2011), the MAX effect;
- Ang, Hodrick, Xing and Zhang (2006), the idiosyncratic volatility puzzle;
- Boyer, Mitton and Vorkink (2010), expected skewness.

A model trained to find next month's top 2% movers will learn that volatile stocks move most, because
they do. Our predictions:

- (a) M10 and P3 picks sit in the top decile of volatility and MAX within their universes.
- (b) Volatility-type features carry most of each model's attribution.
- (c) Neither model's picks beat the universe after factor adjustment (V2 tests this part).
- (d) P3 does worse than M10, because lottery demand is strongest in penny stocks.

## Tests (on the cached weekly panel, signal dates 2019-2025, the same walk-forward as M10 and P3)

1. **Pick characteristics.** Each week, the within-universe percentile of every pick's vol21, vol63,
   maxret21, price and (for M10) market cap. Reported: the median percentile over all picks.
2. **Attribution.** XGBoost's exact tree SHAP values (`pred_contribs`) from each year's walk-forward
   model on that year's test rows, grouped as follows:
   - volatility: vol21, vol63, volratio, maxret21, gap5;
   - trend: r1, r5, r21, r63, r126, mom, hi52, lo52, up21, rsi14;
   - size and liquidity: ldv20, dvr, lprice, lmcap;
   - news: news7, news30, an_up, an_down, an_net.

   Reported: each group's share of mean absolute SHAP, over all test rows and over the picks.
3. **Placebo A, shuffled labels.** Labels are permuted within each date (numpy
   default_rng(20261015 + date ordinal)) before every walk-forward fit. The top-20 excess should be
   about zero, with about 2% of picks in the top 2%.
4. **Placebo B, stale signal.** The real model's picks from week t are held over the window that
   starts a week later. If the picks' returns come mostly from momentum that has already happened,
   this barely changes the result. If they come from a real short-lived signal, it shrinks.

Pass rules don't apply. Each prediction (a) to (d) is reported as confirmed or not confirmed. A placebo
A result above 1 point a month or a hit rate above 4% means leakage, and M10 and P3 would be rerun
after a fix.

## Amendment 2026-10-15 (after the first run, disclosed)

The first run tripped the leakage flag: M10's shuffled-label picks landed in the top 2% 4.5% of the time
(rule: above 4%), while their mean excess was -0.1% (rule: above 1 point). Before any fix, the rerun adds
two diagnostics: the shuffled-label picks' volatility percentiles, and the top-2% hit rate of the 20
highest-vol63 stocks each week with no model. If that baseline is at or above 4.5% and the placebo picks
sit in the top volatility decile, the hit rate comes from picking extreme stocks, not from future data,
and the flag is resolved without rerunning M10 and P3. Otherwise M10 and P3 are rerun after a fix.
