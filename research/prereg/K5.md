## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

1. **Counterparty.** Institutions that trade in the last half hour (index rebalancing, hedging, and
   fund flows into the close) make the late-day move follow the first half hour's direction. Whoever
   trades the other way at the close is paying for that flow.
2. **Why a $1B fund can't take it away.** It can, and many do, which is why any edge should be small.
   The strategy holds 20 minutes a day on one ETF, so its capacity is not the constraint.
3. **Published?** Gao, Han, Li & Zhou (2018, *Journal of Financial Economics*, "Market intraday momentum"):
   on SPY the first half hour's return predicts the last half hour's return, stronger on high-volume and
   macro-news days. The sample ends in 2013. Whether it still holds is the question.

**Prediction written now:** after costs, the bot's daily return is above its coin-flip twin by a small
amount (under 0.05% a day) with a Newey-West t below 3 over six months. A pass is unlikely. Gross returns
are positive more often than not.

## Rules (fixed now; the bot takes no discretion)

All times are US Eastern. Runs happen every 10 minutes, at minute 7 of each ten-minute block, during market
hours on weekdays. Decisions use only data available at the run.

- **Signal.** `r1` = open price at the 10:00 mark / the 09:30 open, minus 1. The 10:00 mark is the open of
  the first 1-minute bar at or after 10:00. Alpaca SIP bars (end time now - 16 minutes), `adjustment=raw`.
  `r1` is known from the first run after 10:00 that day.
- **Entry.** The first run at or after 15:30 (usually 15:37). If `r1 > 0`, buy SPY long; if `r1 < 0`, sell
  SPY short. If `r1 == 0` or the run can't get a price, no trade that day. Price: the latest real-time
  IEX trade at the run (same feed approved for bot C only; see the amendment note below). Size: all
  equity (1x, no leverage).
- **Exit.** The first run at or after 15:57. Price: the latest IEX trade at that run. This leaves a
  three-minute gap before the 16:00 close; it is part of the rule, not a choice made later.
- **One round trip a day** at most. Half-days are skipped (no 15:57 run).
- **Runs outside the window** (before 15:30 or after 15:57) only log the signal and the holding value.

## Controls and benchmarks (same day, same times)

- **A-coin.** The same entry and exit times, the same size, and a direction chosen by a coin flip seeded
  with the date (`random.Random(f"{SEED}-{date}")`, seed 20261018). It tests whether the signal adds
  anything beyond time-of-day exposure to SPY.
- **HOLD.** Buy SPY at the first run and hold. The plain benchmark.

## Costs

- 0.1% per side, as K2 and K4 (`k2.FEE`), and shorts pay the same 3% a year borrow (`k2.BORROW`) pro rata.
  That equals the 0.2% round trip in config/risk.yaml.

## Primary test (one, forward only)

- Daily net return of book A minus book A-coin, Newey-West t with 5 lags (PROTOCOL rules 11-12,
  `core.stats.screen_pass`).
- **Pass:** mean difference > 0, t ≥ 3, and the daily difference's bootstrap 95% CI above zero, after
  at least 120 trading days (about six months). Before then the verdict is "no conclusion".
- **Fail:** any other outcome after 120 trading days.
- Reported against HOLD as well, but not as the pass rule.
