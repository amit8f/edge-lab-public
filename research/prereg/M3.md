# M3: residual momentum (main track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any residual or return of this idea was computed. From plan v2, which
had M3 forward-only; it gets a historical screen first, because M4's data cache makes a screen cheap
and a failed screen saves a year of waiting. Phase-2 count at registration: 10 (main 7, news 1,
penny 2). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 25.

## Question

Do the 25 stocks with the strongest residual momentum (Blitz, Huij & Martens 2011: momentum in the
part of the return the Fama-French factors don't explain) beat the equal-weight universe over the
next month, after costs?

M4 used plain 12-1 momentum as one of six families in a composite (failed). M3 is a different signal
tested on its own.

## Universe, dates

M4's universe (`research/prereg/M4.md`) on each signal date t = the last trading day of each month,
2016-01-29 .. 2025-08-29 (116 dates). Every 1-month window ends before 2025-10-01.

## Data

Monthly total returns from Alpaca daily bars (`adjustment=all`), month-end to month-end. Fama-French
3-factor monthly returns and the risk-free rate from Kenneth French's data library, downloaded once
when the screen runs; its sha256 is saved with the results.

## Signal (no fitted parameters beyond the per-stock regressions)

For each stock at t: regress its monthly excess return on the 3 factors over the 36 months ending at
t (at least 24 months present, otherwise no score). Take the residuals of months t-11 .. t-1 (11
months, skipping the most recent month t). Score = sum of those residuals / their standard deviation.
The 25 highest scores are the picks.

## Entry, exit, costs

Entry at the next trading day's open, exit at the close of the next month's last trading day; a
series that ends early exits at its last close. Equal weight. Cost 0.2% round trip per pick per month
(as if every pick turned over).

## Primary test

Per t: mean return of the 25 picks minus the mean return of the whole eligible universe minus 0.2%.
Statistic: the mean over the 116 months, Newey-West t with 3 lags, moving-block bootstrap 95% CI
(blocks of 6 months, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261015).

**Screen passes** only if the mean > 0, the HAC t >= 2.0 and the CI's lower bound > 0.

## Control

25 stocks drawn at random from the same date's universe (numpy default_rng(20261015 + date ordinal)).

## Exploratory, decided now (never reported as findings)

Market-only residuals (CAPM); plain 12-1 momentum in the same setup (the comparison M3 is meant to
beat); top 50; 3-month hold; per year; score deciles.

## Forward test (confirmatory, only after a screen pass)

From the first month-end after the screen passes: the 25 picks and a 25-stock random control are logged
each month-end into the hash-chained ledger; the paper book holds the top 10 under the main track's
risk rules with a month-end time exit. After 12 logged months, the primary statistic on the forward
picks. No real money.

## Stop rule

A failed screen ends M3.

## Data QA (equity-data-qa)

Funnel counts; the 20 largest monthly pick returns checked against raw bars; residual-score
percentiles by year; the factor file's date range covers every regression window; the random control
near zero.

## Amendment (2026-10-15, before any result of this idea exists)

Amit approved the project-wide rules 10-15 in `research/PROTOCOL.md`. For this screen that means:
- The pass bar is a HAC t >= 3.0 (was 2.0), with the other conditions unchanged.
- It must also show a monthly alpha > 0 with t >= 2.0 on the Fama-French 5 factors plus momentum
  (Newey-West errors, same lags as the primary test; `core/stats.py`).
- It reports the deflated Sharpe ratio, with the phase 1 + phase 2 idea count from `research/IDEAS.md`
  as the number of trials.
