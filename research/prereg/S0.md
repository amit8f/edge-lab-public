# S0: how strong news events move in the first two hours after entry (descriptive, not counted)

Written 2026-11-20, before any S0 number exists. Source: Amit, 2026-11-20, asked for a fast trade that enters
expecting a big move within about an hour, with a take-profit and stop that adjust; then chose "S0 first" on the
decision card (describe our own events before registering S1, S2 or S4). Research behind it:
`/mnt/project-files/reports/Sub hour momentum trades.md`. Descriptive only: no rule, no pass or fail, idea count
unchanged (176, 191 with phase 1).

## Question

After NX3's strong news calls (the NX3-ALL-HI events), how far does price go in the call's direction minute by minute,
how far against it, and how much does waiting 5, 15 or 30 minutes before entering change that? The answers fix the
time horizon, entry delay and stop width that S1 and S2 would register; they are not themselves a result.

## Data (fixed now)

- Years 2019-2023 only (the choice years). 2024 to Sep 2025 stays unread so S1 and S2 can be tested on it; the
  holdout from 2025-10-01 is untouched.
- Events: NX3's walk-forward models (refitted each year, NX3.md), real events that fire with confidence
  c = |e| / cut >= 1.5, uncapped, side from NX3. Control: as many of the same day's real events at random, given the
  same sides (NX3's control).
- Prices: SIP 1-minute bars, adjustment=raw, of the event's session. Entry at the open of the first bar at or after
  NX3's entry minute plus a delay D in {0, 5, 15, 30} minutes.

## Measures

For each delay D, events and control apart, pre-open news (NX3 entry at 9:31) and intraday news apart, longs and
shorts apart:
- the signed return (side x price change from entry) at 5, 15, 30, 60, 90 and 120 minutes (close of the last bar
  that starts before that minute; the session's last bar if earlier): mean, median, share above 0;
- best and worst signed excursion within 60 minutes (bar highs and lows): median and the 75th / 95th percentiles;
- first touch within 60 minutes of +3% in the call's direction vs -1.5% against it (a bar touching both counts as the
  stop): share up first, stop first, neither; also +2% / -1% and +5% / -2.5%.
- counts per cell and events per day.

No costs are charged in S0 (it describes moves); S1 and S2 would charge config/risk.yaml.

## Result (2026-11-20, run 44740814602; descriptive, 2019-2023 only, no costs)

`research/results/S0/S0_paths.json`. 7,651 strong events (c >= 1.5), 6.9 a day; 85% are pre-open news entered at 9:31.
Signed move (side x price change), mean, events vs the random control:

| entry delay | 5 min | 15 min | 30 min | 60 min | 120 min | right way at 60 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | +0.21% | +0.32% | +0.41% | +0.46% | +0.47% | 52% |
| 5 min | +0.09% | +0.19% | +0.24% | +0.30% | +0.30% | 52% |
| 15 min | +0.06% | +0.09% | +0.11% | +0.16% | +0.15% | 52% |
| 30 min | +0.01% | +0.02% | +0.05% | +0.08% | +0.10% | 50% |
| control, delay 0 | -0.02% | +0.01% | +0.02% | +0.05% | +0.03% | 50% |

- The move comes early: about 90% of the 2-hour move is there after 30 minutes, and waiting 5 minutes costs a third
  of it; waiting 15 minutes, two thirds. Shorts move a bit more than longs (+0.52% vs +0.40% at 60 minutes).
- Paths are wide: within 60 minutes the median best move is +2.5% and the median worst -2.0% (control +0.9% /
  -0.9%). A +3% target vs -1.5% stop is hit first 32% vs 49% of the time (19% neither); +2% / -1%: 35% vs 54%.
- Intraday news (1,119 events) is different: entered at once, longs lose (-0.05% at 60 minutes); after a 15-minute
  wait they gain (+0.25%). Shorts work either way. Thin cells (252-845).
- Direction is right only about 52% of the time at any horizon; the gain comes from the right calls moving further
  than the wrong ones.

What it sets for S1 / S2 (each still needs its own registration before 2024-25 is read): no entry delay for pre-open
news, a 30-60 minute horizon, and the 15-minute wait only for intraday longs. Costs (0.2% round trip main, 2% penny)
take a large share of +0.4-0.5%.
