# W2: published short-seller reports, with a stricter target check (news track)

Registered 2026-10-24, before any W1a or W2 return was computed (W1a stopped at its hand check). Amit's
decision in the failure review grill (`research/FAILURE_REVIEW_2026-10-24.md`). Phase-2 count at
registration: 50 (news 8). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 65.

## Why

W1a (`prereg/W1.md`) found only 23 of its 50 hand-checked events right, mostly in the big-outlet group
(1 of 21: it tagged buyers, sellers and denials as rumoured targets). The short-seller group was better
(11 of 14). Its three misses were all articles tagged with a second company that the report wasn't about
(a Spruce Point post on Oatly tagged BYND; a Hunterbrook short on UWM tagged RKT; a Citron data note
tagged ZM). These fixes come from the hand check, not from any return.

## Rule (everything not listed is W1a's short-seller group rule)

- **Group:** W1a's short-seller list only. The other four groups are dropped.
- **One company:** the article is tagged with exactly one symbol.
- **Named in the headline:** the headline contains that company's ticker (as a whole word, or as $XYZ /
  "(NYSE: XYZ)") or its short name (`adaptive/lanes.py` `short_name`, matched whole-word and
  case-sensitively, as scout lane 19).
- **Short words:** as W1a (short report, short seller, short position, fraud, report, short), direction
  always short.
- Entry 30 minutes after the article (W1a), exit at the close of trading day +5, signed stock minus SPY,
  minus C0's round trip and borrow. Same-day control as W1a. Window 2016-01-01 .. 2025-09-23 (exits
  before 2025-10-01).

## Data QA first

All events if there are fewer than 50, else 50 random (numpy default_rng(20261024)), are checked by hand
before any return: right company, really a new short report or campaign post by a listed short seller.
**Fewer than 80% right stops W2.**

## Primary test and pass

W1a's group test: >= 100 events, mean tradable return > 0 with a date-clustered Newey-West t >= 3.0, the
block-bootstrap CI above 0, FF5 + momentum alpha t >= 2.0. **Fewer than 100 events is inconclusive**, and
then W2 goes to a forward log instead: every new qualifying article from the news collector, judged by
PROTOCOL rule 20 after 60 trading days and 30 events.

Prediction: about 65% fail or come out inconclusive (short reports are rare in Benzinga's feed, and the
30-minute wait may miss most of the drop).
