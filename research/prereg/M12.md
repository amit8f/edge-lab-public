# M12: boom model with volatility, size and industry taken out, run as a real portfolio (main track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any result of this idea was computed. Requested by Amit: "test whether
M10 and P3 are just high-volatility bets: adjust the features to remove volatility, size and industry,
re-run ... model a real portfolio with sizing, sector caps and costs from quoted spreads". This is a new
idea built after M10's result, so it's post-hoc to M10 (disclosed). Phase-2 count at registration: 16
(main 10). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 31 (amended the same day, before any result: the first version said 14
and 29 because another thread's L1 and B0 landed on main first).

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10; amendment added before any result)

M12 was committed at 13:02 UTC, after rule 10 took effect at 13:00, so it owes these three answers.

1. **Counterparty.** None named. Once volatility, size and industry are removed, whatever is left in
   price, volume and news features has no group of traders we can point to who systematically sell too
   cheap. The best candidate is slow reaction to analyst actions (M6's lead), where the other side is
   investors who don't read revisions.
2. **Why us.** Weak. The weekly top 20 sits in liquid $5+ stocks that a $1B fund can also trade.
3. **Decay.** The features (short reversal, momentum, analyst revisions) are all published; McLean and
   Pontiff (2016) put the post-publication loss at about a third to a half.

This answer is why the expectation below is a fail. M12 is a diagnostic of M10 first and a trading
idea second.

## What we expect (written before looking)

The lottery-stock literature (see `research/prereg/V5.md`) says that, once volatility is taken out,
little short-horizon predictability is left in these price and news features, apart from the known
one-month reversal and the analyst-revision drift that M6 hinted at. Our expectation is a fail. M12
runs anyway because it answers a clear question: was M10 anything more than "buy volatile stocks"?

## Universe, dates, data

Same as M10 (`research/prereg/M10.md`): M4's filters on the weekly panel, signal dates 2017-01-06 on,
yearly walk-forward 2019-2025, and no label window reaching 2025-10-01.

Industry: Fama-French 12 industries from each company's SIC code in SEC's EDGAR submissions data,
mapped with French's published SIC ranges. The SIC code is the company's current one, not
point-in-time (disclosed; SIC codes rarely change).

## Neutralised features and label

On each signal date, independently:

1. Every M10 feature's within-date percentile rank is regressed by OLS on the vol63 rank, the
   log-market-cap rank and the 12 industry dummies. The residual replaces the feature. vol63 and lmcap
   themselves are dropped, since their residuals are zero.
2. The 21-day return is regressed the same way. The label is y = 1 when the residual return is in the
   top 2% of that date, else 0.

## Model

M10's XGBoost exactly: the same hyperparameters, seed and yearly walk-forward. No tuning.

## Portfolio (fixed now)

Each week:

- take the 20 highest-probability stocks, skipping any stock whose industry already has 4 picks;
- weight them in inverse proportion to vol63, capped at 10% each, with the excess spread pro rata;
- **Cost.** Each pick pays 0.2% round trip. If V4 has finished when M12 runs and V4's measured mean
  cost for M10's picks is higher, that number is charged instead. The cost used is recorded.

## Primary test

Per week: the weighted 21-day return of the picks, minus the equal-weight universe mean, minus costs.
This weekly series is regressed on the window sums of FF5 plus momentum, exactly as in
`research/prereg/V2.md` (Newey-West, 6 lags).

**Passes** only if all three hold:

- the intercept > 0;
- its t >= 3.0;
- the moving-block bootstrap 95% CI of the intercept-plus-residual series (blocks of 8, 10,000
  draws, seed 20261012) has a lower bound above 0.

The deflated Sharpe ratio with 31 trials (amended from 29 with the count above) is reported. A screen pass would only send M12 to forward
paper logging.

## Control

Each week, 20 random stocks (numpy default_rng(20261012 + date ordinal)) under the same industry cap
and weighting. The same statistic is computed.

## Exploratory (reported as such)

- the same model without neutralisation, but with the portfolio rules, which isolates the portfolio
  rules' effect;
- equal weights;
- no industry cap;
- per-year results;
- feature attribution (SHAP groups as in V5).
