# T1: buy December's biggest penny losers for the turn of the year (penny track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any of this idea's data was downloaded. Amit's choice from
`rethink/PRO_NEXT_2026-10-15.md`. Phase-2 count at registration: 16 (main 9, penny 4, news 2,
cross-track 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 31.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- **Counterparty:** US taxable investors who sell losing stocks in December to book the capital loss,
  whatever the price. In small, thinly held stocks that selling pushes the price below value, and it
  stops in January (Roll 1983; Reinganum 1983; later work finds the effect concentrated in small,
  illiquid losers).
- **Why us:** the losers are $1-5 stocks with small dollar volume; a fund can't put money to work in
  them over a few days. Our positions are a few hundred dollars.
- **Decay:** published in 1983 and widely known; in large stocks it's mostly gone. Expect at most a
  fraction of the old numbers, and costs (2% round trip for pennies) may take all of it.

## Universe and dates

- Year y: formation day F = the 5th-to-last trading day of December y; entry at the open of the last
  trading day of December y; exit at the close of the 5th trading day of January y+1.
- Universe on F: P1's penny universe (`research/prereg/P1.md`: Alpaca common stock, delisted kept, raw
  close $1-5, median 20-day raw dollar volume >= $500k, >= 60 bars in the current series), plus at
  least 200 bars in year y up to F in the same series.
- Losers: the bottom decile of the universe by adjusted return (`adjustment=all`) from the last close of
  year y-1 to the close on F (ranked; ties by symbol).
- Historical years: y = 2016 .. 2024 (9 turns of the year; every exit is before the 2025-10-01 holdout).
  Forward: y = 2026, paper traded in the penny sleeve.

## Trade and control

Equal weight, long only. A stock without a bar at entry is dropped; one that stops trading before the
exit is closed at its last close, kept in the average. Net return = the adjusted close at exit over the adjusted
open at entry (`adjustment=all`), minus 1, minus 2% (penny round trip, `config/risk.yaml`).

Control: for each year, as many stocks as there are losers, drawn at random (numpy
default_rng(20261017 + y)) from the rest of the universe on F, traded with the same rule.

## Primary test

Per year: mean net return of the losers minus mean net return of the control. Over the 9 years: the
mean, a t-statistic (Newey-West, 1 lag) and a 95% bootstrap CI over years (10,000 draws, seed
20261017). **Pass** (PROTOCOL rules 11-12) only if the mean > 0, t >= 3.0, the CI's lower bound > 0,
and the alpha test below gives alpha > 0 with t >= 2.0. The deflated Sharpe ratio is reported with the
IDEAS.md count as trials.

Alpha (rule 11, adapted because 9 yearly points can't carry a 6-factor regression): per loser-year,
the net return minus its year's control mean, regressed on the Fama-French 5 factors plus momentum
summed over the holding window (daily French files, `core/stats.window_factors`), standard errors
clustered by year. This adaptation is stated before results and needs no further approval.

With 9 years the power is low; a pass needs a large and steady effect. A failed screen ends T1 and the
2026 paper trade is not made. A pass means a December 2026 paper trade, reported as one more year
(descriptive; one year can't confirm anything).

## Pipeline check (PROTOCOL rule 14)

T1 uses the same Alpaca SIP daily bars, asset list and delisted handling as the M3 panel, so it counts
only if M3's pipeline replication (`research/REPLICATION.md`) passes. Its own reduction (year-to-date
return, entry, exit) is checked by hand on 20 random loser-years, written to
`research/results/T1/T1_rows_check.csv` before the result is reported.

## Reported alongside (not tests)

Each year's numbers; the losers' return before costs; the same test for $5+ stocks with a median
20-day dollar volume of $500k-$5M (descriptive: a stand-in for small caps, since T1 loads no market caps); the median dollar volume of the
losers (capacity).
