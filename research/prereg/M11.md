# M11: calculated-risk boom picks, M10's boom model filtered by value and quality (main track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any result of this idea was computed. Requested by Amit, who asked for
picks that are "underpriced or could explode in the near future while taking a lot of things into
consideration". Phase-2 count at registration: 8 (main 7). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 23.

**Disclosure.** This idea exists because M10 failed in a specific way: its picks were the most
volatile stocks, with 13% landing in the month's top 2% but 10% falling 30% or more. The filter
below is chosen to cut that downside. That makes it post-hoc to M10's result; it is tested on the
same 2019-2025 out-of-sample weeks, so a pass is a candidate for a forward test, nothing more.

## Rule (no new fitted parameters)

Universe, signal dates, label window, costs, walk-forward XGBoost and its settings: exactly M10's
(`research/prereg/M10.md`). On each signal date:

1. Candidates: the 100 stocks with the highest M10 probability.
2. Fundamentals score, each a percentile within that date's full universe, using M4's definitions
   (SEC facts counted from their filing date): quality = mean rank of gross profit / assets and
   net income / assets; value = mean rank of earnings / price and book / price; buybacks = rank of
   minus the 12-month change in log shares. Score = mean of the three that exist; at least two must.
3. Picks: the 20 candidates with the highest score (ties by M10 probability).

## Primary test

Weekly series 2019-01-04 .. the last date whose 21-trading-day window ends before 2025-10-01: mean
21-day return of the 20 picks minus the universe mean minus 0.2%. Newey-West t with 6 lags, moving-
block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 8, 10,000 draws, seed 20261012). **Passes** only if mean > 0,
t >= 3.0 and the CI's lower bound > 0.

Reported with it, decided now: the share of picks that fall 30% or more and that rise 30% or more in
21 days, next to M10's top 20 on the same weeks.

## Controls

Same-day random 20 from the universe (numpy default_rng(20261012 + date ordinal)); and, to isolate
the filter, 20 drawn at random from the same 100 candidates (default_rng(20261013 + date ordinal)).

## Exploratory, decided now

Candidate pools of 50 and 200; quality only; value only; buybacks only; picks minus M10's top 20
(paired by week); per year; versus SPY.

## Stop rule

A fail ends M11. A pass goes to a forward paper test under its own pre-registration; the dashboard
shows the result either way.
