# P4: insider cluster buys in penny stocks, with a fixed stop-loss and take-profit (penny track)

Registered 2026-10-19, before any result of this idea was computed. Requested by Amit, who asked for a
strict $100-250 paper account that tries to catch penny stocks that are about to rise, with a defined
stop-loss and take-profit. Chosen from the literature review in the project files
(`reports/Small account boom stock rules.md`). Phase-2 count at registration: 31 (penny 5). Phase 1 +
phase 2 = 46.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- Other side: sellers of thin, ignored penny stocks (retail holders and funds exiting small positions)
  who don't read Form 4 filings, while insiders who buy with their own money know more about the firm
  (Lakonishok & Lee 2001; Cohen, Malloy & Pomorski 2012, "opportunistic" buyers about +0.82%/month).
- Why a $1B fund can't take it: positions of a few thousand dollars in $1-5 stocks with ~$1M daily
  dollar volume don't matter to a large fund, and its own buying would move the price.
- Published: yes. Expect decay of roughly half after publication (McLean & Pontiff 2016). Our own M5
  (insider buying plus buybacks, $5+ stocks, 6 months) failed at t 0.72; this test differs by price band,
  holding period, the cluster and opportunistic conditions, and the exits.

## Question

After costs, do penny stocks bought after a cluster of opportunistic insider purchases, held up to 21
trading days with a fixed stop-loss and take-profit, beat a same-day random penny stock of the same
volatility traded with the same exits?

## Universe and dates

- Universe on each signal date: raw close from $1 to under $5, median 20-day dollar volume >= $1M,
  common stocks named in SEC Form 3/4/5 filings by that date, NYSE/Nasdaq/NYSE American only (no OTC),
  delisted stocks kept, one row per company (highest dollar volume).
- Bars: Alpaca SIP daily bars, `adjustment=raw` for price, dollar volume and fills; `adjustment=all`
  for returns across splits. A >15-calendar-day gap ends a series; zero-volume bars dropped.
- Signal dates: 2017-01-01 on. No exit window may reach 2025-10-01 (holdout).

## Signal

On filing date F (the SEC acceptance date; Forms accepted until 10pm ET keep that date):
1. Two or more distinct reporting owners (officers, directors or 10% owners) each filed an open-market
   purchase (transaction code P, non-derivative) in the 10 calendar days ending F.
2. The cluster's total purchase value is at least $25,000.
3. At least one buyer is "opportunistic": no code-P purchase by that owner in the same calendar month in
   each of the three prior years (Cohen, Malloy & Pomorski 2012).
4. Exclusions: the stock rose more than 30% over the 5 trading days ending F; or it is in the top decile
   of the penny universe by largest one-day return over the prior 21 trading days (MAX effect); or a
   reverse split, an offering (424B prospectus or S-1/S-3 effective) or a delisting notice (8-K item 3.01)
   was filed in the 90 days ending F.
A stock re-enters only after its previous trade has closed.

## Entry, stop-loss, take-profit, exit

- Entry: the open of the first trading day after F. Skipped if that open is more than 1% above F's close
  (a limit order that would not fill).
- Stop-loss: -20% from entry. Take-profit: +40% from entry. Checked on each day's low and high. If the
  open gaps past a level, the fill is that open. If a day touches both, the stop counts first.
- Otherwise exit at the close of the 21st trading day after entry. A delisting exits at the last close.

## Costs

2.0% round trip (penny cost in `config/risk.yaml`). The measured penny spread from C0 (0.65%) is
reported beside it.

## Control

For each trade, one random stock from the same day's penny universe in the same volatility decile (60-day
daily return volatility), with no qualifying signal that day, traded with identical entry, stop,
take-profit, exit and costs. numpy default_rng(20261019 + date ordinal).

## Primary test

Weekly series (trades grouped by entry week): mean of (trade return minus its control's return). Newey-
West t with 4 lags and a moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 8 weeks, 10,000 draws, seed 20261019).
**Passes** only if the mean > 0, t >= 3.0, the CI's lower bound > 0, the Fama-French 5 + momentum
monthly alpha of the trades is > 0 with t >= 2.0 (rule 11), and no single stock provides more than 20%
of the total net profit. The deflated Sharpe ratio uses 46 trials.

A pass only starts the forward paper test (its own pre-registration). Fewer than 100 trades means the
screen is reported as inconclusive, not passed.

## Exploratory, decided now

Hit rates of the stop and the take-profit; the same rule held 21 days with no stop or take-profit;
-25%/+50% levels; one-insider buys; by year; median trade; without the best trade; the measured 0.65%
cost; market-model CAR with the Kolari-Pynnonen correction.

## Paper account, if the screen passes

$250 Alpaca paper account, about $100 per position, at most 2 positions, whole shares only, skip a
signal if fewer than 20 shares are affordable, pause new entries after a 10% drawdown from the peak.

## Stop rule

A failed or inconclusive screen ends P4 as a paper-trading candidate.
