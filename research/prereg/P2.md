# P2: buy penny stocks after guidance raises, with dilution and pump filters (penny track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any trade of this idea. From plan v2. Phase-2 count at registration: 9
(main 6, news 1, penny 2). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 24.

## Question

Is buying $1-5 stocks after a guidance raise profitable on paper over 5 days, after costs and stops,
once offerings and pumps are filtered out?

Phase 1's I-13 forward test measures the raw 5-day return after these events. P2 adds fixed filters
and trades it. Forward-only, because the evidence is in-sample.

## Universe

P1's penny universe (`research/prereg/P1.md`).

## Event (phase 1's frozen rule, unchanged)

C4 guidance raised (sign from the event's first article), from breakout-scanner commit 111b71d,
`catalyst/rules.py` with `pipeline/p27c_classify.py`, copied without changes. Events cluster per stock
within 2 days.

## Filters (fixed now)

Skip the event if any of these holds on the signal night:
1. Dilution: an A8 offering event (P1's definition) for the stock in the prior 90 calendar days.
2. Pump: the stock's close-to-close return over the 5 trading days up to the event day is above +50%.
3. Reverse split: an A9 event in the prior 180 calendar days.

Skipped events are logged with the reason.

## Trade

Signal: the nightly job that first sees the event. Entry: long at the next open. Exits (paper book,
penny settings): stop at 3 x ATR(20) below the fill, capped at -25%, checked on the close and filled
at the next open; time exit at the close of the 5th trading day. Costs: 2% round trip. Sizing: 0.25%
of the account at risk to the stop; P1 and P2 share the penny track's 5 positions.

## Control

For each trade, a stock drawn at random (numpy default_rng(20261016 + date ordinal)) from the same
night's penny universe with no C4 or A8 event in the prior 20 trading days, bought with the same rules.

## Primary test

Per closed trade: net return minus its control's net return. Analysis at 100 closed trades, or on
2027-12-31 if fewer: mean, t clustered by entry week, 95% CI. **Pass** only if there are at least 100
trades, the mean > 0, t >= 2.0 and the CI's lower bound > 0. Fewer than 100 at the ceiling date:
UNDERPOWERED, never a pass (guidance raises are rare among penny stocks, so this is the likely outcome;
it is registered anyway so the trades are counted honestly).

## Reported alongside (not tests)

Paper P&L of the sleeve; events seen, filtered (by reason) and traded; the unfiltered raw return
(the I-13 comparison).

## Stop rule

A 10% sleeve drawdown pauses P2 for review. A failed or underpowered test ends P2.

## Clarifications (2026-10-15, before any trade)

P1's clarifications 3-10 apply to P2 (with P2's seed 20261016 and its control pool). Also:

1. The dilution filter looks at filings dated in [d - 90 days, d], the signal day included: an offering
   form or an 8-K with item 3.02, from SEC's submissions record of the company, plus any A8 event the
   job itself logged for the stock.
2. The pump filter uses adjusted closes: C(d) / C(5 trading days before d) - 1. A stock without both
   bars is not filtered by this rule.
3. The reverse-split filter runs the frozen A9 bar rule on each day of [d - 180 days, d] for the stock,
   plus any A9 event the job logged.
4. Filters are checked in the order listed; a skipped event records the first rule it fails.
5. P1's clarification 11 (logged borrow fees) does not apply; P2 is long only.
