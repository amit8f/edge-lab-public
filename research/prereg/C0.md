# C0: what trading really costs at our size (measurement, not an idea)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any quote was downloaded. C0 tests no edge and is not counted as an idea;
it can't pass or fail. Its numbers replace guesses: `config/risk.yaml` charges 0.2% round trip for
$5+ stocks and 2% for penny stocks, and nobody has measured either.

## Who pays whom

A market order pays half the bid-ask spread to the market maker on the way in and again on the way out.
For orders of $500-$2,000, half the spread is the main cost; market impact at that size is small next to
it. So C0 measures the quoted spread when our trades would actually happen.

## Sample

- Days: 24 trading days drawn at random (numpy default_rng(20261015)) from 2025-01-02 .. 2025-09-30.
- Stocks: Alpaca common stocks (`core.instruments.classify`, delisted kept), bucketed on the prior
  trading day's raw close and 20-day median raw dollar volume:
  - penny: $1-5, at least $500k
  - small: $5+, $2M-10M
  - mid: $5+, $10M-50M
  - large: $5+, $50M+
- 100 stocks per bucket per day, drawn with default_rng(20261015 + day ordinal).

## Measure

The NBBO quote in force at 09:31, 09:35, 10:00, 15:50 and 15:59 ET: the last SIP quote in the 2 seconds
before the instant, or in the 60 seconds before it when the 2-second window has none. A quote counts
only if bid > 0, ask > bid and the spread is under half the midquote. Half-spread = (ask - bid) / 2 /
mid.

Also, for the same stock-days, the Corwin & Schultz (2012) estimate from that day's and the prior
day's highs and lows, so the history can be costed where quotes aren't available.

## Reported

For each bucket and instant: quote coverage, and the 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles of the half-spread
in basis points. For each bucket: the round trip of entering at 09:35 and leaving at 15:59 (median and
75th percentile), and the Corwin-Schultz median.

## Use

Each track's cost becomes the 75th percentile round trip of its bucket(s) (main: small, mid and large;
penny: penny), rounded up to the next 0.05%, when that is higher than the current value. It is never
lowered. The new values apply to ideas registered after C0's result; screens already registered report
both their registered costs and the C0 costs, and keep their registered verdict.

## Limits

Spreads only: no market impact, opening auction prices or borrow fees. Borrow fees have no free history.
From November, the nightly job logs Alpaca's easy-to-borrow flags forward.
