# L1: how early is the official record? First-public-moment lags (news track)

Registered 2026-10-15, before any filing time, headline or return of this idea was looked at. Amit's
"news clues" request, as rewritten in the deeper plan. Phase-2 count at registration: 14 (main 9,
penny 3, news 2). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 29.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- **Counterparty:** traders who react to the Benzinga or newswire headline rather than the filing.
  A filing is public the second EDGAR accepts it. If the headline follows hours later, someone who
  reads the filing trades before them.
- **Why us:** a fund can read EDGAR in real time, but for $50M-$2B companies the profit is too small to
  bother with. A $5k account can take it.
- **Decay:** EDGAR-reading is old news for large caps (machine downloads of filings are well
  documented). For small caps, the lag is the open question L1 measures.

## Events

EDGAR filings by companies in our universe (common stock, raw close >= $1, median 20-day raw dollar
volume >= $500k on the prior day; penny = under $5, main = $5+), accepted 2023-01-01 .. 2025-09-24.
Each category has a sign fixed now (the direction a reader expects the stock to move):

| Category | Rule | Sign |
|---|---|---|
| Offering | S-1, S-3 (original), 424B1-424B5 | - |
| Late filing | NT 10-K, NT 10-Q | - |
| Delisting notice | 8-K item 3.01 | - |
| Auditor change | 8-K item 4.01 | - |
| Officer or director departure | 8-K item 5.02 | - |
| Activist stake | SC 13D / SCHEDULE 13D (original, not /A) | + |
| Insider open-market buy | Form 4 with a code P purchase by an officer or director | + |

Up to 400 filings per category and price group (penny, main), drawn at random (numpy
default_rng(20261015)), with the acceptance time from each filing's header.

## Measures

- **Headline lag:** the time from acceptance to the first Alpaca/Benzinga article naming the symbol,
  created from 60 minutes before acceptance (press releases sometimes come first, giving a negative lag)
  to 3 trading days after.
- **Price move before the headline:** the signed, SPY-adjusted return from the first regular-session
  minute bar that opens at least 1 minute after acceptance to the last minute bar before the headline
  (1-minute SIP bars, `adjustment=all`). Filings accepted outside the session start at the next open.
- **Tradable return:** the signed, SPY-adjusted return from that same entry to the close of the next
  trading day, minus costs (C0's measured round trip for the stock's bucket, or 0.2% / 2% before C0
  exists), and minus borrow on negative-sign categories (3% / 20% a year).

## Primary decision (per category and price group)

A category becomes a candidate for a forward-tested trading idea only if all of these hold:
1. The median headline lag is at least 60 minutes.
2. The tradable return's mean is > 0, with a t >= 3.0. Errors are clustered by entry date (a
   date-level mean, then Newey-West with 2 lags), and the moving-block bootstrap 95% CI (blocks of 5,
   seed 20261015) is above 0.
3. There are at least 100 events.

The 14 category-group tests are counted together as this one idea, and each is reported. A candidate
gets its own pre-registration with a forward test before any trade; L1 itself trades nothing.

## Control

The same entry and exit times applied to a stock drawn at random from the same day's universe and
price group (default_rng(20261015 + date ordinal)), with the category's sign.

## Data QA

20 random events per category checked by hand (right company, acceptance time matches EDGAR, the
headline is about that filing). Report lag percentiles per category, the share with no headline in 3
days, and the control near minus costs.

## Stop rule

No candidate means L1 ends; the forward logs (`core/leads_sec.py`) keep running for later ideas.

## Clarifications (2026-10-15, before any filing, headline or return was looked at)

The code is `tracks/news/l1_study.py` (workflow `l1-study`, manual runs only).

1. Filings come from each universe company's EDGAR submissions record (forms, filing dates, 8-K items).
   The acceptance time of each *sampled* filing comes from its header, as registered.
2. "Insider open-market buy" uses SEC's quarterly insider data sets (DERA): original Form 4s with a
   code P, acquired, purchase by a reporting owner marked officer or director. The CIK -> ticker map is
   the same data sets' point-in-time map (the latest Form 3/4/5 naming the ticker, within 2 years).
3. Price group and universe come from the trading day before the filing date (raw daily bars). A CIK
   with two eligible tickers that day uses the one with the higher dollar volume.
4. A filing with no headline within the window has an infinite lag: it counts as "at least 60 minutes"
   in the median, and in the share with no headline.
5. The entry bar is the first regular-session 1-minute bar whose start is at least 1 minute after
   acceptance (whole minutes, rounded up), up to the 15:59 bar; the exit is the last bar of the next
   trading day. SPY uses its bars at the same times. Borrow on negative-sign categories is charged for
   the calendar days from entry to exit.
6. The control is drawn from the universe of the trading day before the entry day, same price group,
   not the event's stock, with one default_rng(20261015 + entry date ordinal) per date, drawn in order
   of filing date and accession.
7. Rule 11's CAR: a market model on daily adjusted returns, estimated on trading days -250 .. -30
   before the entry day (at least 100 returns), CAR over the entry day and the next, signed, with the
   BMP t and its Kolari-Pynnonen correction (mean pairwise correlation of the last 100 estimation
   residuals). It is reported per cell; the decision rule above is unchanged.
