# A1: short penny stocks whose annual report shows earnings-manipulation red flags (penny track)

Registered 2026-10-23, before any of this idea's data was pulled or computed. Amit's pick (top 3 of
`planning/BOOKS_REVIEW_2026-10-23.md` in the project files). Phase-2 count at registration: 42 (main 20,
penny 7, news 7, cross-track 7, crypto 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 57.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

- **Counterparty:** buyers who take reported earnings at face value. Profits built on receivables
  growing faster than sales, falling margins hidden by capitalised costs, or earnings far above cash
  flow tend to be reversed later, and the price falls when they are (Sloan 1996 on accruals; Beneish
  1999 and Beneish, Lee & Nichols 2013 on the M-score; Schilit, *Financial Shenanigans*).
- **Why us:** the effect is strongest in small, thinly covered firms that funds can't short in size,
  and borrow there is scarce. That cuts both ways: the same scarcity may stop us shorting, which is why
  borrow decides the forward test.
- **Decay:** published 1996-2013 and used by short sellers, mostly in larger stocks. Prediction written
  now: about 65% this fails, and a pass may be unusable if most flagged names can't be borrowed.

## Universe and dates

- Formation day t = the last trading day of each month, from 2016-12-30 to the last month whose exit is
  before 2025-10-01.
- Universe on t: P1's penny universe (`research/prereg/P1.md`: common stock, delisted kept, raw close
  $1-5, median 20-day raw dollar volume >= $500k, >= 60 bars), mapped to a CIK as in M4.
- Flag: the company's latest 10-K (or 10-KT) filed in the 3 calendar months up to t (by EDGAR filing
  date) has a Beneish 8-variable M-score above -1.78, computed from SEC XBRL company facts as reported
  in that 10-K and the prior fiscal year's values:
  M = -4.84 + 0.920 DSRI + 0.528 GMI + 0.404 AQI + 0.892 SGI + 0.115 DEPI - 0.172 SGAI + 4.679 TATA
  - 0.327 LVGI. DSRI (receivables over sales), SGI (sales growth) and TATA (income from continuing
  operations minus operating cash flow, over total assets) are required; any other index that can't be
  formed from the filing's tags is set to 1 (no change) and counted. Sales below $1M in either year:
  not scored.
- Each company enters once per 10-K.

## Trade and control

Equal weight, short only. Entry at the next trading day's open after t; exit at the close of the 63rd
trading day from entry, or earlier at the first close 35% or more above entry (penny short stop cap,
`config/risk.yaml`). Net short return = 1 minus exit over entry (`adjustment=all`), minus the 2% penny
round trip, minus 20% a year borrow charged daily. A stock that stops trading is closed at its last
close and kept (a delisting usually helps a short; Shumway delisting returns are not added).

Control: each month, as many stocks as there are flags, drawn at random (numpy
default_rng(20261025 + month index)) from the rest of the universe on t, shorted the same way. A month
with no flags is skipped and counted.

Borrow: Alpaca's easy-to-borrow history isn't available, so the screen can't apply PROTOCOL rule 6 on
past dates. This is stated now. A pass can only go forward with rule 6 applied: the forward book shorts
a flag only if it's easy to borrow on Alpaca that day.

## Primary test

Per formation month: mean net short return of the flags minus the control's. Over all months: the mean,
a Newey-West t (3 lags, for the overlapping 3-month holds), a 95% block-bootstrap CI (blocks of 3
months, 10,000 draws, seed 20261025). **Pass** only if the mean > 0, t >= 3.0, the CI's lower bound > 0,
and FF5 + momentum alpha of the monthly series > 0 with t >= 2.0 (Newey-West). Deflated Sharpe with 57
trials.

A failure ends A1. A pass sends A1 to a Plan v3 shadow book with its own forward rule.

## Pipeline check (PROTOCOL rule 14)

Prices come from the M3/T1 SIP pipeline, so A1 counts only if M3's replication passes. The XBRL step is
new: 20 random flags are checked by hand against the 10-K itself on EDGAR (each of the 8 inputs, filing
date, M-score) in `research/results/A1/A1_flags_check.csv` before the result is reported. More than 2
wrong M-scores out of 20 stops the screen until the cause is fixed.

## Reported alongside (not tests)

Results before costs and borrow; accruals alone (top decile of TATA) as a second ranking; the share of
flags hit by the stop; the share of the last 12 months' flags that are easy to borrow on Alpaca today
(read once from the asset list when the report runs).
