# K2: an AI stock trader with $100, paper only (main track)

Registered 2026-10-16, before the bot made any decision or any return existed. K1 (the same bot on
crypto) ran once, at 10:13 UTC, and was stopped the same morning because Amit wants it on stocks: "I
dont want the bot we built to be in crypto I want it in stocks". K1 has no result and stays counted.
K2 copies @laeshaai's "I gave Claude $100 to trade" bot onto US stocks. Phase-2 count at
registration: 20 (main 12, penny 4, news 2, cross-track 1, crypto 1). Phase 1 + phase 2 = 35.

No real money. No orders are sent anywhere, the paper account included. Fills are simulated in our own
ledger from Alpaca SIP prices.

## Mechanism (PROTOCOL rule 10)

1. **Counterparty.** If a language model reading a few days of hourly bars can call the direction of
   ten mega-cap stocks, it takes money from other short-term traders reading the same public bars.
   No slow or forced seller is obviously on the other side.
2. **Why a $1B fund can't take it away.** Nothing stops them. These are the most traded stocks in the
   world.
3. **Published?** Lopez-Lira & Tang (2023) found ChatGPT adds information when reading news
   headlines, not price charts. Price-only LLM trading agents (FinMem and similar) report backtests on
   periods the models may have seen. Short-horizon direction in mega-caps is close to a coin flip.

**Prediction written now:** neither AI book shows skill against its coin-flip replays; both trail SPY
buy-and-hold after costs.

## Symbols, data, timing

- The AI chooses among SPY, QQQ, NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, META, GOOGL, TSLA, AMD (all main-track
  eligible: $5+, $300M+).
- Alpaca SIP bars with `adjustment=raw` (point-in-time prices, fills), requests ending at now - 16
  minutes (free-plan delay, CLAUDE.md).
- Runs at minute 7 of every hour from 13:00 to 21:00 UTC on weekdays. A run trades only when its
  price time (now - 16 minutes) is inside that day's regular session (Alpaca calendar). That is about
  six or seven decisions a day.
- The fill price is the close of the last 1-minute bar at or before now - 16 minutes. The model sees
  only data up to that same moment, so the fill uses no information it didn't have.
- Fractional shares (Amit's broker allows whole shares only; $100 can't hold whole shares of most of
  these, so this is disclosed as a difference from real life).

## Books (all start with $100)

| Book | What it is |
|---|---|
| A | **Her setup, on stocks.** Gemini 2.5 Flash (free key, temperature 0) reads the last 7 sessions of 1-hour bars for the ten symbols and its open position, and answers long/short/close/hold for one symbol with one line of reasoning. One position at a time, exposure 1.25x equity (her 25% margin x 5x), no stop. |
| B | **Our changes.** Same model, plus 60 daily bars; exposure 0.5x equity; a 5% stop; told that holding cash is a good answer unless the daily trend and recent bars agree. |
| A-coin, B-coin | Coin-flip twins: same symbol and timing as A or B, side drawn at random (seed 20261016). |
| HOLD | Buys $100 of SPY on the first run and never trades. |
| TREND | No AI: holds SPY (1x) while its last daily close is above its 20-day average, otherwise cash. |

Gemini 2.5 Flash's training cutoff (2025-01-31) is before every date in the test.

## Costs (main track, `config/risk.yaml`, plus two K2 additions)

- 0.1% of notional on each side (the main track's 0.2% round trip).
- Shorts pay 3% a year borrow on notional (main track `borrow_per_year`).
- Exposure above 1x pays 8% a year margin interest on the borrowed part (K2 addition).
- Stops and margin calls are checked on every 1-hour bar since the last check. A bar that opens past
  the level fills at its open (gaps count). Margin call: long equity below 25% of position value,
  short below 30% (Reg T maintenance); the position is closed at that level and the rest is kept.
- A failed or unreadable model answer is "hold" and is logged.
- Everything the model saw, its raw answer, the prompt's SHA-256, every fill and every book's equity
  are logged each run in the hash-chained ledger (branch `state-ai`, `ai/ledger.jsonl`).

## Primary test (2027-04-16, six months after the first run)

For each AI book: replay its logged round trips 10,000 times with each trip's side drawn at random
(seed 20261016), using the logged bars, the same sizing, stops, margin calls and costs. p = share of
replays ending at or above the book's actual equity. **Skill is shown** only if the book made at least
30 round trips and p < 0.05 after Holm across A and B. Fewer than 30 round trips: "no conclusion".

## Secondary and exploratory (never reported as findings)

Every book against HOLD (SPY), TREND and cash; maximum drawdown; margin calls; win rate; average hold;
share of hours invested; equity at 1, 2 and 3 months; how often A and B agree.

## Stop rule

A book below $5 stops trading and its result is final. A pass is a hypothesis for a new pre-registered
test on other stocks, never a reason to trade real money (that needs the owner's written change to
PROTOCOL.md).
