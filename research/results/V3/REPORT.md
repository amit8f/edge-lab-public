# V3 report: can M8's forward test reach a verdict?

Pre-registration: `research/prereg/V3.md`. Run 38762468648. Running idea count: phase 2 16, total 31.

## Verdict: no

M6's historical edge (+0.50% per 21 days, sd 4.3%) is an information ratio of 0.41 a year. Reaching 80%
power at t >= 2 would take **49 years** of forward data. The chance that M8 passes at 52 weeks even if
the edge is real is 5.6% (4.4% at 26 weeks, 7.8% at 104).

Part B tested M6's rule at 21 days on 452 historical weeks with whole-universe statistics:

| Statistic | Mean | t | Years for 80% power |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top 20 vs universe | +0.20% / 21d | 0.60 | 306 |
| Random 20 (control) | -0.18% | -1.63 | never |
| Rank IC | 0.008 | 1.04 | 95 |
| Net-direction spread | -0.11% | -0.66 | never |

The pre-registered decision: no statistic reaches 80% power within 2 years, so M8 stays a record and no
new idea is registered on the whole-universe scores. M8's weekly log keeps running; its result will be
read as a description, not a test.
